# About MyBookshelf

MyBookshelf keeps a reading shelf of the books you want to read and checks
each one against your library’s own catalog. Free, no account required, no ads.

## What you can do

- Open your reading shelf at mybookshelf.cloud. Everything happens on that one
  screen: your books, the search, the barcode scanner and your library.
- Search by title, author or ISBN, or scan a book’s barcode with your phone’s
  camera.
- Choose the library you borrow from. Use **Change** beside its name to choose
  another one at any time.
- See the answer in words under each book: **In your library**, **Not in your
  library** or **Couldn’t check right now**. “Couldn’t check” means the
  library’s catalog did not answer; it never means the book is missing.
- Find your next read among the suggested books on your reading shelf.
- Make a free account if you like. Log in with a link we email you, or with a
  password. The books already on your device join your account when you sign
  in, and your reading shelf is the same on your phone and your computer.
- On a computer, your library, account and settings sit in a column on the
  left, and a book opens beside your reading shelf.

## How it works

- **Find a book.** Search by title, author or ISBN, or scan the barcode with
  your phone. Add it to your reading shelf.
- **Choose your library.** Pick the library you borrow from. We read its public
  catalog, the same one on its website.
- **See if it’s there.** Each book says “In your library” or “Not in your
  library”. When we can’t check, we say so.

## Built to be trusted

- No account required and no ads. Your reading shelf stays in your browser, or
  in a free account if you make one.
- Location is used only when you choose “Libraries near me”.
- Open source: the code and the library list are public.
- Independent. Libraries set their own borrowing rules.

## Corrections and questions

Readers and libraries can ask for details to be corrected or removed. Write to
us or open an issue with the library’s name and a source for the correction.
To ask for a library to be added, use “My library isn’t listed” in the app.
