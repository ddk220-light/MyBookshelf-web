# About

MyBookshelf helps you find a book and see whether your library has it. Search
by title, author or ISBN, or scan a barcode, and keep the books you want on
your own shelf. Choose your library once, and each book shows whether its
catalog lists it. MyBookshelf is free, has no advertising, and is not run by
any library.
