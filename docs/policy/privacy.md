# Privacy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE].

## Who we are

MyBookshelf is a free, independent website and web app at mybookshelf.cloud.
It is run by Deepak Ravikumar, India. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean
MyBookshelf. We are not a library and are not run by any library.

## Scope

This policy covers the MyBookshelf website and web app at mybookshelf.cloud:
your reading shelf, the public pages, and the optional account. Libraries,
book services and other websites you open from MyBookshelf have their own
privacy policies.

## Information we collect

**Your account (optional).** You never need an account to search, scan a book
or check a library. If you create one, we keep your email address, a
scrambled form of your password (never the password itself), your reading
shelf, your chosen library and your **Viewed before** list (the newest 50
books you opened while signed in). To log in, you can ask for a log-in link
by email instead of typing your password: the link works once, only for 15
minutes. We use your email address to send that link, to confirm your
address, and for security and account messages, never for marketing.

**Information stored in your browser.** Without an account, your reading
shelf, Viewed before list, chosen library and reading light are kept in your
own browser, not on our servers. When you sign in, the books on your reading
shelf in that browser are added to your account, so nothing is lost; the
browser remembers which books it has already added. If the browser and your
account have different libraries, your account's library is kept. Signed-in
cookies keep you logged in; they hold no reading data.

**Information sent to answer you.** To show whether your library has your
books and to suggest books, the app sends us your chosen library and the
identifiers of some of your books. We use them for that answer only and do
not keep them with anything about you. A list of books related to one book is
the same for everyone, so our hosting provider's cache and your browser may
keep that list, under that book's identifier, for up to eight days.

**Anonymous usage information.** When you search, we record the words you
searched for (a book, or a library or place name), the library involved, what
happened (found, not found, couldn't check), how many results came back and
how long it took. We also count how often each suggested book is opened and
saved. We keep no account ID, no IP address and no device identifier with any
of this, so we cannot tell who searched, and search words are never put in
web addresses. We also count page visits, which site sent you, the type of
device and browser, and the country, using Vercel Web Analytics, which sets
no cookies. None of this is linked to an account or a person.

**Library answers.** When we check a library, we keep the answer ("found" or
"not found") for that library and book for six months, so we do not have to
ask the library again. A check that fails is never stored as an answer. These
answers hold nothing about you.

**Library requests and problem reports.** If you ask us to add a library, we
keep the library name and city you typed, and a count of how many times it
was asked for. If you report a problem, we keep your message, the screen you
were on, and the library and book shown. With either, you may add your name
and email so that we can reply. Both are optional, and we delete them after
90 days. Please do not put other personal details in a message.

**Location and camera.** "Libraries near me" sends your location to our server
once, to sort the list of libraries. We do not store it; we store only the
library you choose. If you type a town or city instead, it is looked up
through our server. The barcode scanner works on your device: camera images
are not sent to us or saved.

**Abuse protection.** To limit automated misuse, we keep a scrambled code made
from your IP address for up to one day. It cannot be turned back into the
address. We also limit how often sign-in, log-in links and password resets can
be tried.

## How we use information

- To show your reading shelf and whether your library has each book.
- To keep your account working: signing in, log-in links, merging your reading
  shelf at sign-in, and showing the same reading shelf on every device.
- To send the emails you ask for and the account messages we must send, such
  as a confirmation that your account was deleted.
- To find and fix what is broken or slow, using the anonymous usage
  information.
- To add libraries, answer requests and reports, and correct library details.
- To protect MyBookshelf and the library catalogs from misuse.

## What we never do

- We never sell or rent information, or share it for advertising.
- We never show advertising.
- We never link the anonymous usage information or any log to an account.
- We never put your search words in web addresses or analytics.
- We never keep IP addresses or device identifiers in our records, or store
  your location.
- We never ask for library card numbers or PINs.

## Service providers

| Service | What for |
|---|---|
| Vercel | Hosting the website and server, scheduled clean-up, a small storage area used when deleting accounts, and anonymous visit counts |
| Neon | Databases: one for accounts, one for library answers, requests, reports and the anonymous usage information |
| Resend | Sending account email: log-in links, address confirmation, password resets and account messages |
| Open Library and Internet Archive | Book details and covers |
| Google Books | Book details and covers |
| Photon (Komoot), using OpenStreetMap data | Finding places when you type a town or city |
| Library catalogs | Checking whether a library has a book. Our server asks the library's own catalog system (or its provider, such as BiblioCommons or Calil) directly. It sends the book's details or your search words, never anything about you. |
| Google Search Console | Seeing how people find MyBookshelf in Google Search. It adds nothing to our pages. |

These services receive normal network information, such as an IP address,
when they handle a request, and may keep their own short-term logs under their
own policies.

## Your choices and rights

- You can use MyBookshelf without an account, and without giving your
  location or camera.
- You can see and change your email and password, and log out, in Settings,
  then **Account**.
- You can delete your account in Settings, then **Account**, then **Delete my
  account**. This deletes your email, password, reading shelf, chosen library
  and Viewed before list, and clears what MyBookshelf keeps in that browser.
  Live data is removed within 24 hours and backup copies expire within 30
  days.
- You can clear what is stored in your browser through your browser's site
  data settings.
- You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to
  correct or delete it, by writing to the address below. We will answer within
  30 days.

The anonymous usage information, library answers and library requests are not
linked to you, so they cannot be found or deleted by account.

MyBookshelf.
