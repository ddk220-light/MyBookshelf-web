# Privacy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. MyBookshelf is run by Deepak Ravikumar, India.

This covers the MyBookshelf website and web app at mybookshelf.cloud.

## What changed

Until [EFFECTIVE DATE], MyBookshelf did not store the words you searched for.
From [EFFECTIVE DATE], we keep searches and library checks in an anonymous log,
without your account, IP address or any device identifier. We use it to find
what is broken or slow. If you have an account, your **Viewed before** list is
saved to it automatically.
We also count visits with a cookie-free analytics tool.

## What we collect and why

**Your account (optional).** Your email address, a scrambled form of your
password, your saved books, your chosen library and your **Viewed before**
list. We use these to show your shelf on any device you sign in on. Signing in
is never needed to search or to check a library.

**Without an account.** Your shelf, Viewed before list and chosen library are
kept in your own browser. We do not receive them.

**Viewed before.** When you are signed in, each book you open is added to your
Viewed before list in your account, newest first, up to 50 books. You can
clear it at any time. It is deleted with your account.

**Anonymous visit counts.** We count page visits, which site sent you, the
type of device and browser, the country, and totals such as how many searches
happen each day. This uses Vercel Web Analytics,
which sets no cookies. It is never linked to an account or a person.

**Anonymous search and library-check log.** When you search, we record the
words you searched for (a book, or a library or place name), the library
involved, what happened (found, not found, couldn't check), how many results
came back and how long it took. We keep no account ID, no IP address and no
device identifier with it, so we cannot tell who searched. Search words are
never put in web addresses.

**Which libraries have which books.** When we check a library, we keep the
answer ("found" or "not found") for that library and book for six months, so
we do not have to ask the library again. A check that fails is never stored as
an answer. These answers do not include anything about you.

**Library requests.** If you tap "Request to add" for a library that is not
listed, we keep the text you typed and a count of how many times it was asked
for. Nothing about you is kept with it.

**Problem reports.** If you send a report, we keep your message, the screen
you were on, and the library and book shown. We do not attach your account or
email. Please do not include personal details in the message.

**Location.** "Libraries near me" uses your location once to sort the list.
We do not store it. We store only the library you choose.

**Camera.** The barcode scanner works on your device. Camera images are not
sent to us or saved.

**Stopping abuse.** To limit automated misuse, we keep a scrambled code made
from your IP address for up to one day. It cannot be turned back into the
address.

## What we do not collect

- We do not keep IP addresses or device identifiers in our records.
- We do not store your location.
- We do not show advertising.
- We do not sell or rent data, or share it for advertising.
- We do not ask for library card numbers or PINs.

## Who processes data for us

| Service | What for |
|---|---|
| Vercel | Hosting the website and server, scheduled jobs, a small storage area used when deleting accounts, and visit counts |
| Neon | Databases: one for accounts, one for library answers, requests, reports and the anonymous log |
| Resend | Sending account email (verification, password reset, security notices) |
| Open Library and Internet Archive | Book details and covers |
| Google Books | Book details and covers |
| Photon (Komoot), using OpenStreetMap data | Finding places when you type a town or city |
| Have I Been Pwned (Pwned Passwords) | Checking a new password against known leaks. Only the first five characters of a scrambled form of the password are sent. |
| Library catalogs | Checking whether a library has a book. Our server asks the library's own catalog system (or its provider, such as BiblioCommons or Calil) directly. It sends the book's details or your search words, never anything about you. |
| Google Search Console | Seeing how people find MyBookshelf in Google Search. It adds nothing to our pages. |

These services receive normal network information, such as an IP address,
when they handle a request, and may keep their own short-term logs under their
own policies.

## Deleting your account

Go to the side panel, then **Account settings**, then **Delete account**.
This deletes your email, password, saved books, chosen library and Viewed
before list. Live data is removed within 24 hours; backup copies expire within
[BACKUP DAYS] days. A short record that holds no email or reading data is kept
for 35 days so that a backup restore cannot bring a deleted account back.

Deleting your account does not clear books saved in a browser without an
account. Clear those in your browser's site data.

The anonymous log, visit counts, library answers and library requests are not
linked to your account, so they cannot be found or deleted by account.

## Children

You must be 18 or older, or have a parent's or guardian's permission, to use
MyBookshelf.
