                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_Rules">GENERAL RULES<br /> <br />•Identity Card is compulsory for getting access to the library.<br />•Library users entering the library shall register in the Online Library visitors System installed at the entrance and record feedback.<br />•The users will not leave any valuables at the Library/Reading Room. Library is not responsible for any loss of personal belongings.<br />•Identity card is not transferable. Users are advised not to borrow books for others in their names.<br />•Members shall observe silence in the library so that other users are not disturbed.<br />•Members shall not smoke, or spit in any part of the Library.<br />•Library resources such as books, journals, and electronic materials, etc. are costly and are often rare. They are for the benefit of not only the present but also for the future members of the library. Therefore, readers should not deface, mark, cut, mutilate or damage library resources in any way. Books borrowed should be protected from rain, dust, insects, etc. Members are responsible for any damage caused by them to the books or any other property belonging to the Library and shall be required to pay the penalty imposed upon them by the Librarian or any other competent authority.<br />•Before leaving the circulation counter, member should satisfy themselves as to whether the material lent to them is in sound condition. If not, they should immediately bring the matter to the knowledge of the library staff at the issue counter; otherwise, they (users) are liable to be held responsible for replacing the material or paying such compensation as fixed by the Librarian.<br />•Books removed from the shelves by students, if not required for reference, should be kept on the table nearest to them. Students shall abstain from shelving books themselves.<br />•The newspaper(s) should be folded properly after reading and kept back in the designated place.<br />•Beverages and eatables are not allowed inside the library.<br />•All users are requested to keep their mobiles switched off or in silent mode in the library. The use of mobile phones is strictly prohibited on the library premises.<br />•As the library is a place of self-study and research, members should maintain an atmosphere of dignity, peace, and silence within the library premises.<br />•OPAC stations are meant for searching the resources subscribed/purchased by the library. Accessing personal emails, Facebook, Chatting, etc. on such computers is not allowed.<br />•Library users are advised to enter the library premises in UNIFORM designated by the College.<br />•Members should keep the library informed of any change of Telephone number/address during the period of their membership.<br />•No visitor or guest is permitted to use the library without the prior permission of the Librarian.<br />•Upon any infringement of the Library rules members shall forfeit the privileges of admission and membership in the Library.<br />•Suggestions regarding the purchase of books/other materials, subscriptions, improvement of library services, complaints, etc., may be given to the Librarian or to be dropped in the suggestion box.<br />•At all times, library users will respect prevailing IPR/copyright rules for any library material issued to or downloaded by them. At any given point, only up to 20% of documents can be photocopied. Cover-to-cover photocopying of any document is prohibited.<br />•The Librarian has the power to cancel the membership and refuse admission to anyone who violates the rules and regulations of the Library or indulges in any other type of misconduct.<br />•For the issue of books/other reading material, the borrowers should present the same along with their Identity card to the staff at the circulation counter. After necessary checking, the Library staff will issue the book by scanning the barcode on the identity card and on the book and return the book to the borrower.<br />•Books should be brought physically for return and renewals. A renewal over telephonic/verbal requests/written communication is not entertained.<br />•Borrower is fully responsible for the books/any other material borrowed in his/her account. Hence, members are advised not to sub-lend the materials borrowed from the Library.<br />•In exceptional cases, a document may be recalled before the due date if required urgently by the library. Failure to respond promptly to such requests may lead to the suspension of library privileges.<br />•If the borrower fails to return the book(s) within a reasonable time of its due date as indicated in the recall notice/due date, the borrower is liable to pay the applicable charges.<br />•For the issue of new arrivals, priority will be given to members who recommended that book.<br />•Members proceeding on long leave or leaving the station for any other purpose exceeding three months should return the documents that are borrowed by him/her before leaving the Institute.<br />•It is advised to all the members to download our library mobile app.<br />Periodicals and Journals<br />•Current issues and back volumes of periodicals/journals are not issued to the members.<br />Reservation<br />Books (that are issued to other members) can be reserved through library OPAC or through the mobile app.<br />On receipt of the reserved books, the member will be communicated about their availability in the library. However, such transactions must be settled within two months from the date of reporting the loss.<br />Recovery of Books Lost by Members<br />If a book or any of the above material is lost or mutilated beyond usable condition, the following rules will apply:<br />•The book or any of the above material has to be replaced with the latest edition of that material;<br />•Pay the cost of the latest edition of the book or any other reading material along with an additional charge of Rs. 50/- towards administrative costs;<br />•If the book/any other material is out of print or found to be of rare value, then four times the cost of the book has to be paid by the member;<br />•If the book/any other material is reported (in writing) as lost/misplaced, the overdue charges are not levied in such case from the date of the report until the same is replaced (it must be resolved within two months). However, if the member fails to resolve the issues within the prescribed time frame, applicable rules relating to the penalty/overdue charges will apply.<br />•If a book/any other material belonging to a multi-volume set is lost, the borrower will replace the entire set at his/her own cost along with an additional charge of Rs. 50/- per volume towards the security tag and administrative cost;<br />Clearance Certificate<br />All students/staff etc. who leave the Institution must obtain a Clearance Certificate or No Due Certificate from the Librarian.</span>
