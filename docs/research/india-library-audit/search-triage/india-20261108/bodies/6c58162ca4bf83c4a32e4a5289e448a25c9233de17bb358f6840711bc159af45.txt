                                                <div class="d-flex justify-content-between">
                                                    <p class="card-title">About Library</p>
                                                </div>
                                                <hr class="bg-gradient-success" style="height: 1px; width: 100%; margin: 0px; border-top: 0px; padding: 0px;" />
                                                <hr class="bg-light" style="height: 1px; width: 100%; margin: 0px; border-top: 0px; padding: 0px;" />

                                                <p style="text-align: left; font-family: Arial;">
                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_Intro">The library, as an integral part of RIESI, plays a crucial role in facilitating various activities aimed at fostering a deeper comprehension of cutting-edge techniques and approaches in the realm of English Language Teaching and Teacher Education. With its purposeful design, the library strives to promote knowledge acquisition and dissemination, providing valuable resources to educators and researchers in these fields. By offering access to a diverse range of materials, the library empowers individuals to stay informed and up-to-date with the ever-evolving landscape of English language pedagogy, ultimately contributing to the advancement of teaching practices and the professional growth of educators.</span><br />
                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_Intro2">The library, an integral component of RIESI, is engaged in a diverse array of activities aimed at fostering a comprehensive understanding of the most recent techniques and approaches in English Language Teaching (ELT) and Teacher Education. Our library boasts an outstanding collection of over 13,000 books, journals, and various materials encompassing all facets of ELT and related subjects, meticulously curated to cater to the unique requirements of our esteemed users. In line with modern trends, the library is fully computerized and adheres to the Open Access System, ensuring seamless access to resources. Our state-of-the-art facility features a dedicated reference section and a spacious reading room, offering a serene environment for studying and researching.</span>
                                                </p>

                                                <p style="text-align: left; font-family: Arial;">
                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_Objective">OBJECTIVES OF THE RIESI LIBRARY:<br /><br />The library at RIESI has several objectives that guide its functioning and services. These objectives include:<br /><br />. Knowledge Acquisition and Dissemination: The library aims to provide a rich collection of resources, including books, journals, and other materials, to facilitate the acquisition and dissemination of knowledge in the field of English Language Teaching (ELT) and Teacher Education. It ensures that users have access to the latest research, techniques, and approaches in these areas.<br /><br />. Support for Research and Scholarship: The library strives to support research activities by offering a wide range of scholarly resources. It provides a platform for researchers to explore and access relevant literature, enabling them to conduct thorough investigations, contribute to the academic community, and enhance the quality of their work.<br /><br />. Professional Development: The library recognizes the importance of continuous professional development for educators and teachers. It offers resources that assist in expanding their knowledge, refining teaching methodologies, and staying updated with the evolving trends in ELT and Teacher Education. The library also organizes workshops, seminars, and other events to foster professional growth and collaboration.<br /><br />. Information Literacy and Skills Development: The library aims to enhance information literacy skills among its users. It provides guidance and assistance in locating, evaluating, and effectively utilizing information resources. Through workshops and training programs, the library equips users with the necessary skills to critically analyze and synthesize information, enabling them to become competent researchers and lifelong learners.<br /><br />. Creating a Conducive Learning Environment: The library endeavors to create a welcoming and conducive learning environment for users. It offers a spacious and comfortable reading room, equipped with modern facilities for accessing audio and video materials. The library staff are available to provide assistance and guidance, ensuring that users can optimize their experience and make the most of the available resources.<br /><br />Overall, the library's objectives revolve around empowering users with knowledge, supporting research endeavors, fostering professional development, promoting information literacy, and creating an environment that facilitates effective learning and collaboration.</span><br />
                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_Objective2"></span>
                                                </p>

                                                <p style="text-align: left; font-family: Arial;">
                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_History">ORIGIN: <br /><br />The library was established concurrently with the institute's inception in 1963, with a key objective of catering to the needs of ELT professionals who were to be trained at the institute.</span><br />
                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_History2"></span>
                                                </p>

                                                <p style="text-align: left; font-family: Arial;">
                                                    <span id="Lbl_Lib_Services">RIESI LIBRARY SERVICES:<br /><br />The library offers a range of services to cater to the diverse needs of its users. Some of the key services provided by the library include:<br /><br />. Access to Resources: The library houses a vast collection of books, journals, periodicals, research papers, and other materials related to English Language Teaching (ELT) and Teacher Education. Users can borrow these resources or access them within the library premises.<br /><br />. Reference and Research Support: The library offers reference services to assist users in finding relevant and reliable information. Librarians are available to provide guidance on research strategies, citation styles, and navigating databases. They can also help users locate specific resources or provide recommendations based on their research needs.<br /><br />. Interlibrary Loan: If a required resource is not available in the librarys collection, interlibrary loan services may be available. Through collaboration with other libraries, users can request resources from partner institutions, expanding the range of materials accessible to them.<br /><br />. Digital Resources: The library provides access to digital resources, including e-books, online journals, databases, and educational platforms. Users can access these resources remotely, allowing for flexible and convenient learning and research opportunities.<br /><br />. Audio-Visual Facilities: The library may offer audio-visual facilities, such as listening stations, viewing rooms, or multimedia equipment, to support language learning and research activities. These facilities enable users to engage with audio and video materials relevant to ELT.<br /><br />. Workshops and Training: The library organizes workshops, training sessions, and orientation programs to enhance users information literacy skills, research abilities, and familiarity with library resources. These sessions aim to empower users in effectively utilizing library services and conducting scholarly research.<br /><br />. Study Spaces: The library provides dedicated study spaces, including individual study carrels, group study rooms, and quiet reading areas. These spaces offer a conducive environment for concentration, collaboration, and academic discourse.<br /><br />. Online Catalog and Research Tools: The library maintains an online catalog that allows users to search for resources, check availability, and manage their accounts. Additionally, it may provide access to research tools, citation management software, and online databases, facilitating efficient research processes.<br /><br />. Document Delivery and Photocopying: The library may offer document delivery services, allowing users to request copies of specific articles, book chapters, or other materials not available within the librarys collection. Photocopying and printing services may also be available for users convenience.<br /><br />. Assistance and Guidance: Library staff are available to assist users with their inquiries, provide guidance on resource selection and usage, and address any library-related queries. They are knowledgeable about the librarys services and resources, ensuring a helpful and supportive experience for users.<br /><br />These services collectively aim to support users in their academic and professional pursuits, fostering a rich learning and research environment within the library.</span><br />
