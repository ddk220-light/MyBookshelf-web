# Recheck of unreachable Indian catalog hosts, 15 November 2026

A gentle live recheck of the groups that could not be reached in the
[connector follow-up](CONNECTOR-FOLLOWUP.md). Full answers, times and
headers are in [recheck-20261115-results.json](recheck-20261115-results.json);
the one book check is in [recheck-20261115-koha-results.json](recheck-20261115-koha-results.json).
Failures are kept. A failed or blocked check means "couldn't check", never
"not held" and never "empty".

## Method

**From this machine (16:24–16:29Z).** `tools/catalog-hardening/host-recheck.mjs`
read, per host: robots.txt, the member's catalog page, then one app search for
`history` under the app's request budget and robots policy. It stops at the
first policy refusal. The first pass treated a robots.txt 503 as the site's
answer and stopped. For KALNET and IDEAL that 503 was the execution egress
proxy's own "remote connection failure" page, so a second pass (16:27Z)
continued to the catalog page and the app search. A host that answered got the
existing one-book runner (`koha-run.mjs`,
[manifest](../../../tools/catalog-hardening/manifests/recheck-20261115-koha.json)).

**From the app's servers (16:29Z).** One `POST https://mybookshelf.cloud/api/v2/holdings/check`
per group, with `{libraryId, bookKey: "v1:isbn:9788173711466"}` (an ordinary
ISBN book key). Three requests in all, not repeated.
