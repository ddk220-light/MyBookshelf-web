Libraries

The Xavier University Libraries (XUL) is more than a cluster of libraries; it connects people with information by providing diverse resources and services to the academic community.

Each library has an immense collection of books, journals, films, maps, databases, and more. XUL develops and maintains a broad array of information resources, including TLC Catalog Search, article and indexing databases, e-journal subscriptions, and specialized collections crossing subjects, formats, and genres. XUL also provides computing resources, including public computers, audio-visual rooms, and software services.

SLMIS

Access your Student Lifecycle Management and Information System (SLMIS) to check your schedule, room assignments and grades.

eLearn

eLearn is XU’s online classroom where students make use of the new media to pursue academic excellence and virtual connectivity.

XUTV

Launched in 2015, Xavier University Television (XUTV) is a production frontlined by the Development Communication Department.  Episodes focus on XU’s thematic goals on environment, peace, food security, governance and health, among others.

Crusader Yearbook

The Crusader Yearbook is the official annual compendium of Xavier University – Ateneo de Cagayan, capturing the gist of the students’ university experience in a creative and quality package.

Weather Station

XU Weather Station provides you with precise weather updates via the WeatherLink Network – Vantage Pro Plus Data.
