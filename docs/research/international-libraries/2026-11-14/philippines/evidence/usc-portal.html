Downtown Campus - Bernard Bonk Library

Bernard Bonk Library

The Bernard Bonk Library is a subject discipline library situated at the 2nd floor of the Arthur Dingman building in Downtown Campus with a 239 seating capacity, catering the School of Business and Economics which is composed of following departments: Accountancy, Business Administration, Economics and Hospitality and Tourism. It offers resources and services focused on business, economics, finance, hospitality and tourism and other related fields. Typically upkeep academic, research, entrepreneurship and professional development for students, faculty members, researchers, administration and industry professionals.

Programs and Services

  • Circulation Counter
    • Check-out, checked-in, renewal and reservation/request.
  • Reference & information
    • Queries (onsite and online)
  • Knowledge Navigation Center
    • Use of computer terminals with a fee.
  • Printing and Photocopying
    • Printing and Photocopying service at the circulation section.
  • Database searching assistance
    • Provides instruction and assistance on how to access subscribed online databases and Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC).
  • Information Literacy Program
    • Level 1, 2, 3 and 4.
  • Current awareness
    • Eboard and new acquisitions display.
