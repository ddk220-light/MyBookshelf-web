VISION

To be the University’s premier repository of knowledge, empowering minds that shape and serve the nation

MISSION

We serve as the University’s key instrument in advancing teaching, research, and extension, in pursuit of truth, knowledge, and wisdom

GOALS

”LEAD”

  1. Leverage digital platforms, AI tools, and e-resources to transform learning and knowledge access.
  2. Enhance library spaces into dynamic learning commons that foster teamwork, creativity, and innovation.
  3. Advance research through data management services, digital repositories, and training.
  4. Develop literacy programs. outreach initiatives, and active engagement with DACUN, MASLINET, and other partners to advance nation-building.
