Information Resource Facilities


The University Library System is composed of the Main Library in the Batac campus and the unit libraries and reading centers in the colleges. The Main Library houses thousands of volumes of learning materials in print, electronic, and other formats. It also has special collections on specific subjects including those on Ilokano culture. It also houses the American Corner and the Chinese Corner. Located at the first floor of the Main Library building is the MMSU Museum while the second floor houses the Center for Flexible Learning.
