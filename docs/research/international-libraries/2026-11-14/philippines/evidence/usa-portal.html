University of San Agustin-Learning Resource Center Opens 36th Library and Information Services Month

The University of San Agustin Learning Resource Center (USA-LRC) officially opened the 36th Library and Information Services Month (LIS Month) through a celebration highlighting the role of libraries in connecting knowledge, cultures, and communities.

Anchored on this year’s theme, “ASEAN Libraries: Integrating Networked Knowledge for Culture,” the celebration underscores the importance of libraries as bridges that preserve cultural heritage, promote knowledge sharing, and foster connections among Southeast Asian communities.

The opening program welcomed guest of honor Mr. John Paul Mondejar, Director of the International Relations Office, together with faculty members, staff, students, and friends of the library.

In her welcome remarks, the Learning Resource Center Director highlighted how this year’s celebration beautifully embodies the core Augustinian values:

Veritas

Reflects the library’s commitment to truth, accuracy, and the responsible sharing of knowledge.

Unitas

Emphasizes the importance of collaboration and collective learning in fostering stronger connections.

Caritas

Underscores the spirit of service and compassion that guides the library in making knowledge accessible to all.

Throughout the month, the USA-LRC invites the Augustinian community to explore its curated book displays, participate in various activities, and discover the rich cultures and traditions of ASEAN through the library.

More than a celebration of books and information, the 36th LIS Month serves as a powerful reminder that libraries are vital spaces where cultures meet, connections grow, and knowledge becomes more meaningful when shared.

Happy 36th Library and Information Services Month!
