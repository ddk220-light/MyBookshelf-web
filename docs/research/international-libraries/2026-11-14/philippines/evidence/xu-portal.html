The name "Ateneo" is the Spanish form of the Latin word Athenæum, which refers to a temple dedicated to Athena, the Greek goddess of wisdom, learning, and the arts where poets and scholars gather to read and discuss their work.

In modern contexts, an atheneum (or athenaeum) is an institution, building, or room dedicated to the promotion of learning, literature, and science.
