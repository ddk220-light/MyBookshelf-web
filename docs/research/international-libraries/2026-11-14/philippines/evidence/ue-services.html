UE Department of Libraries Facebook page

 

Your librarians will assist you online via Google Meet every Monday to Friday, 9:00-12:00 noon and 1:00-4:00 p.m.You will be admitted to the G-Meet on a space-available basis. You may always email the library for asynchronous assistance as well. Manila: library@ue.edu.ph or Caloocan: library.cal@ue.edu.ph

Borrowing and returning books are placed at the Book Drop boxes provided at the Recto and Gastambide Gates in Manila and at Samson Road Gate in Caloocan Campus.

The library offers viewer access to physical collections which you can conveniently request and read from anywhere, including book chapters, journal articles, archival documents, reference works, and more.

You may request viewer access to chapters, pages, notes, and annotations of the books.

UE faculty and students can now book an onsite visit at the library every Monday through Friday, from 7:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

eSCRA is an electronic resource of the complete Supreme Court’s decisions from1901 to the present.
