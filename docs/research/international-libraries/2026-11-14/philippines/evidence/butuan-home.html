Welcome to Butuan City Library

The Butuan City Library is a center of learning, research, and community engagement, providing accessible and reliable resources to support education, lifelong learning, and cultural enrichment. With a diverse collection of books, periodicals, digital resources, and reference materials, the library serves students, researchers, professionals, and the public in a safe and welcoming environment. Committed to promoting literacy and informed citizenship, the library offers programs and services that empower the community to explore, learn, and grow.

VISIT

The Butuan City Library is open to students, researchers, and the general public from Monday to Friday (except holidays), 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Visitors are encouraged to review library policies and sign in upon entry to ensure an orderly and productive learning environment.

LIBRARY SECTIONS

The library houses a growing collection of books, local history materials, government publications, and reference works. Given to resources that support education, culture, research, and lifelong learning for the Butuanon community.

ACCESS

Users may browse the library shelves on-site and utilize available reading and study areas. Library staff are on hand to assist patrons in locating materials and maximizing the use of available resources for academic and personal research needs.

REFERENCE SERVICES

Our libraries provide reference and research assistance, helping users identify credible sources, locate information, and use library materials effectively. Guidance is available for students, educators, and researchers seeking reliable and relevant information.

LIBRARY SECTIONS

The Library Collection Section of the Butuan City Library houses a diverse range of books, reference materials, periodicals, and local resources that support education, research, and community learning. It includes general reading materials, Filipiniana, butuan history, and specialized collections designed to preserve knowledge and provide accessible information for students, researchers, and the public.

GENERAL REFERENCES

The General References section provides reliable and authoritative sources such as dictionaries, encyclopedias, atlases, almanacs, and handbooks. These materials are designed for quick fact-checking and background information, supporting research, study, and general knowledge needs.

FILIPINIANA

The Filipiniana section features books, documents, and resources about the Philippines, including its history, culture, literature, politics, and notable Filipino authors. It preserves national heritage and supports research on Filipino identity, society, and achievements.

BUTUANON COLLECTION

The Butuanon Collection contains materials focused on the history, culture, traditions, and heritage of Butuan City and the surrounding region. It includes local publications, historical records, research works, and community documents that preserve Butuan’s identity and support local studies and cultural awareness.

FICTION

The Fiction section offers a wide selection of novels, short stories, and literary works across various genres such as romance, mystery, fantasy, science fiction, and classics. It promotes reading for pleasure, creativity, and imagination for readers of all ages.

CHILDREN'S SECTION

The Children’s Section provides age-appropriate books, storybooks, picture books, and educational materials designed to support early literacy and learning. It encourages young readers to develop a love for reading through engaging stories, colorful illustrations, and fun, informative content.

BIMS SECTION

A specialized section of the Butuan City Library that provides resources on technology, innovation, and creative projects. It offers books, manuals, and materials supporting hands-on learning, digital skills, robotics, and maker activities for students and the community.

BARANGAY SECTION

Barangay Reading Center provides information about the reading centers established in different barangays of Butuan City. It includes barangay profiles, affiliations, and other relevant details to help users learn more about the reading centers in their communities.

LIBRARY SERVICES REQUEST

Library Services Request allows the community to access a range of programs offered by the Butuan City Library, including the Knowledge Hub for research and learning, Innovation Trainings for skills development, Puppetry and Mascot Shows for entertainment and engagement, and Storytelling sessions to promote literacy and imagination among children and youth.

LIBRARY TOUR

Library Tour Services allow students, groups, and visitors to explore the Butuan City Library’s facilities, collections, and programs. The guided tours introduce users to library resources, special sections, and services, promoting awareness, literacy, and effective use of library materials.

LIBRARY OUTREACH

Library Outreach Services provide puppetry and storytelling activities for children and youth to promote literacy, creativity, and a love for reading through fun and engaging learning experiences. These services encourage children to explore stories, develop their imagination, and discover the joy of learning.

INNOVATION TRAININGS

Innovation Trainings at the Butuan City Library provide workshops and hands-on learning in technology, creative skills, and problem-solving. They aim to develop digital literacy, entrepreneurship, and innovative thinking among students, youth, and the community.
