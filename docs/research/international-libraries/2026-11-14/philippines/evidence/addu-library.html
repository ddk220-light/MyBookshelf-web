American Corner Davao

January Kanopy Film Party

LOOK|

“You don’t know about real loss, ’cause it only occurs when you’ve loved something more than you love yourself. And I doubt you’ve ever dared to love anybody that much.”

Such a masterpiece, being shown at the American Corner Davao on January 28, 2026. Where we held our Monthly Film Kanopy, the film featured was Good Will Hunting.

Here, not only do we promote all beloved Pop-Culture films that have been a phenomenon in American Culture or even Pop culture in general, but also their values, and beautiful Screenplays. But also, to create a room of inclusivity, to learn the message of the writing, the passion that goes behind it, and to share and create ideas and emotions that further elevate and spur our understanding of what it is to truly live life. These and more are promoted in the American Corner.

Almost 40 participants of the event, from both Outside schools and Ateneans, as well as American Expats, enjoyed the film. Participants and organizers will be given certificates afterwards.

Philippine-US Friendship Day

LOOK|
On July 4, 2024, the Ateneo International Studies Students Organization (AISSO), in collaboration with American Corner Davao, celebrated Philippine-US Friendship Day by hosting a panel discussion at the American Corner in the Jacinto campus of Ateneo de Davao University. During the event, the panellists discussed relations between Filipinos and Americans with the audience, who were a mixture of American and Filipino students and young professionals. It was hosted by Syed Ampatuan of AISSO and Roni Go of Holy Cross of Davao College.

US-PH Exchanges Roadshow

LOOK|
On July 1, 2024, the United States Government Alumni Association – Davao proudly hosted its inaugural event of this year’s US-PH Exchanges Roadshow at AMYA Polytechnic College. The event joyously celebrated the 247th Anniversary of U.S. Independence, the 69th Philippine-American Friendship Day, and the 75th Anniversary of US-PH People to People Exchanges in the Philippines. Mr. Kevin Gutierrez, coordinator of American Corner Davao, was present during the event to represent and speak about the services and opportunities of the American Corner.
