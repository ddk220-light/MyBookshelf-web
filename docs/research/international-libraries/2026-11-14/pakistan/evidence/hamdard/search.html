History & Founding
The story of Bait-al-Hikmah Library
Origin
Founded by Hakim Mohammed Said Shaheed, a renowned scholar, physician, and philanthropist who dedicated his life to education, health, and the promotion of Islamic heritage in Pakistan.
Name
Named after the legendary Bait al-Hikmah of Abbasid Baghdad — the great 8th-century "House of Wisdom" that served as the world's foremost centre of learning, translation, and scientific thought.
Vision
Established to preserve Pakistan's intellectual and cultural legacy while supporting modern academic research across medicine, law, Islamic studies, humanities, and social sciences.
Today
Now one of the largest academic libraries in Pakistan with over 250,000 volumes, fully integrated with Koha ILS, and home to irreplaceable rare manuscripts and archival collections.
📚
Collections & Heritage
A treasury of knowledge across centuries and languages
Rare Manuscripts

1,738 rare manuscripts in Persian, Arabic, Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, and Turkish — many centuries old and of immense scholarly value.

Scholar Manuscripts

657 manuscripts authored by distinguished Pakistani writers and academics, preserved as part of the national intellectual heritage.

Holy Quran

Photocopies and microfilm copies of the Holy Quran translated into 66 languages — a remarkable contribution to Islamic scholarship worldwide.

Unique Collections

A rare collection of coins, postal stamps, and historical photographs documenting decades of Hamdard Foundation activities.

Membership & Borrowing Rules
Know your borrowing privileges and responsibilities
Membership
  • A valid library membership card is mandatory for all borrowing transactions
  • Membership cards are strictly non-transferable
  • Lost cards must be reported immediately to the circulation desk
  • Cards must be renewed at the start of each academic year
Borrowing Limits
  • Students: 2 books for a period of 14 days
  • Faculty / Teachers: 3 books for 30 days
  • Reference materials, rare books, and manuscripts are for in-library use only and cannot be borrowed
  • Thesis and dissertation copies are non-circulating
⚖
Fines, Penalties & Clearance
Policies to ensure fair access for all library members
Late Returns
  • A fine of PKR 10 per day per item is charged for overdue books
  • Fines accumulate from the day after the due date and must be cleared before borrowing further
  • Members with outstanding fines will have borrowing privileges suspended until dues are settled
Lost or Damaged Books
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced with a new copy of the same edition
  • An additional PKR 100 service charge applies to all lost or damaged items
  • If a replacement copy is unavailable, a cash equivalent of the current market price is accepted
Library Clearance
  • Library clearance is mandatory before issuance of any degree, transcript, or official university document
  • All borrowed books must be returned and all fines cleared before clearance is granted
📋
User Guidelines & Conduct
Maintaining a respectful environment for all researchers and students
General Conduct
  • Maintain complete silence inside all library areas at all times
  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode; calls are not permitted inside
  • Group discussions must be conducted in designated discussion rooms only
  • Respectful behaviour towards library staff and fellow users is expected
Books & Belongings
  • Inspect books carefully for existing damage before borrowing and report it at the desk
  • Personal bags must be deposited at the property counter before entry
  • No food, beverages, or drinks are permitted anywhere inside the library
  • Books must be returned to the circulation desk only — do not re-shelve borrowed items
⚠ Violation of library rules may result in suspension of membership privileges.

Library Services
Comprehensive services supporting research, teaching, and learning
Acquisition

Collection development through purchase, gifts, donations, and exchange programs with national and international institutions.

Cataloguing

Systematic cataloguing and accessioning of all materials via Koha ILS following international standards.

Binding

In-house binding and physical preservation services for journals, theses, and deteriorating volumes.

Reference

Professional reference and bibliographic assistance provided by qualified library staff for research support.

Digitization

Ongoing digitization of rare and fragile materials in the Digital Preservation Lab in collaboration with international partners.

Distribution

Over one lakh books distributed across faculty libraries, research centres, and affiliated colleges under Hamdard University.
