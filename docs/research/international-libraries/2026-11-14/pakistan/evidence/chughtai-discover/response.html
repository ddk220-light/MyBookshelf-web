WELCOME TO CHUGHTAI PUBLIC LIBRARY

 

Chughtai Public Library is an effort to collect, organize, preserve and provide every source of pure knowledge to the yearning common man. It is a resource of antique and contemporary books and journals covering a wide range of subjects including art, culture, science, education, jurisprudence, medicine, and philosophy. It collects information on every subject under one roof. It is a nonprofit organization the main objective of which is to establish a center where students and aspiring scholars of all ages and disciplines from every walk of life and every stratum of society can find an opportunity to learn and carry out research.
