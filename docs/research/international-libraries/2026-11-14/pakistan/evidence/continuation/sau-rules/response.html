Library Rules & Regulations

Membership Eligibility

Only registered students, faculty, and staff of SAU Tando Jam can borrow books. Valid ID card is required for membership.

Library Timings

Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM – 10:30 PM. Closed on Sundays & public holidays. E-resources available 24/7 online.

Borrowing Limits

Students: 3 books for 15 days | Faculty: 5 books for 30 days | Staff: 5 books for 21 days. Reference books are non-loan.

Overdue Charges

Rs. 5 per day per book for late returns. Lost or damaged books must be replaced with the same edition or paid at current market price.

Silence Zone

Mobile phones must be on silent mode. No loud talking, eating, or drinking (except water) is allowed in the library premises.

Digital Resources

E-resources are for academic use only. Copyright violation, bulk downloading, or sharing of credentials is strictly prohibited.

Reference Section

Rare books, journals, theses, and reference materials cannot be taken out of the library. Photocopying is allowed with permission.

Code of Conduct

Maintain discipline and respect for library staff and fellow users. Any misconduct may result in suspension of library privileges.

The library reserves the right to amend these rules at any time. For any clarification or special requests, please contact the librarian.

About Library

The Central Library of Sindh Agriculture University, Tando Jam, is dedicated to providing comprehensive academic resources, digital databases, and a conducive environment for research and learning.
