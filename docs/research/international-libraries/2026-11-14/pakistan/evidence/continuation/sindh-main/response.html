ABOUT

The University of Sindh, the oldest University of the country!

Jamshoro

The University of Sindh, the oldest University of the country, was constituted under the University of Sindh Act. No. XVII of 1947 passed by the Legislative Assembly of Sindh. The Act was subsequently revised and modified in 1961 and later. The Act of 1972 under which the University is presently functioning provided for greater autonomy and representation of teachers.

MESSAGE

The Vice-Chancellor

Professor Dr. Fateh Muhammad Marri

Dear Faculty, Officers, Staff and Students,

It is with profound gratitude and a strong sense of responsibility that I have assumed as Vice Chancellor, University of Sindh, Jamshoro. It is both a privilege and an honor to lead this historic and prestigious institution — one that has stood as a symbol of learning, progress, and enlightenment in Sindh for generations. The University of Sindh has produced luminaries in every walk of life and continues to serve as a place of knowledge and culture in Sindh and Pakistan.
