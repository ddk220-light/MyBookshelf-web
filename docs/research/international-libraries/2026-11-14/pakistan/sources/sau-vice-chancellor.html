Message From The Vice Chancellor

It gives me great pleasure to say that the Central Library of Sindh Agriculture University (SAU), Tandojam, stands as the intellectual heart of our university, bridging traditional learning with modern digital resources. Under my guidance, the library has grown into a complete and dynamic resource center, offering a vast collection of printed books alongside access to thousands of international online journals and databases through the HEC Digital Library.

I am proud to share that our library provides a comfortable and inspiring study environment with dedicated reading areas and fully automated services, ensuring that both our students and faculty have everything they need to excel academically. It is my firm belief that by empowering our researchers with the right knowledge and tools, we can collectively address the challenges of global food security and drive meaningful rural development.

Our rich collection of hundreds of thousands of volumes, including rare textbooks, international journals, and specialized research reports in agronomy, veterinary sciences, agricultural engineering, information technology, and social sciences, reflects our deep commitment to academic excellence and the advancement of agriculture.

Prof. Dr. Altaf Ali Siyal

Vice Chancellor, SAU Tandojam

Digital Library

Welcome to the Digital Library of Sindh Agriculture University (SAU), Tandojam. Our digital library is a modern and easy-to-use platform that gives our students, teachers, and researchers quick and simple access to knowledge from around the world.

We use the Koha Library Management System, which helps us manage all library activities smoothly and allows users to search for books and journals easily through our online system called Web-OPAC. Through the HEC National Digital Library, we provide access to more than 11,000 research journals and thousands of e-books, all available at the click of a button.

Our digital library is especially helpful for Ph.D., M.Phil., and Master's students who need the latest research and scientific information for their studies. We have made sure that our internet connection is fast and reliable so that no one faces any difficulty while accessing these resources.
