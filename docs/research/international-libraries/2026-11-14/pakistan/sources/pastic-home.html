What members get

Koha, hosted for you

A complete Koha library system on the PASTIC cloud. PASTIC installs it, keeps it secure, backs it up every day and answers your questions. About Koha Cloud

A place in PakCat

Your holdings join the national union catalogue, so researchers anywhere in Pakistan can find them and ask for them through inter-library loan.

Digital repository

Open access to indigenous S&T literature, and a home for your own theses, reports and journals. Open the repository

Training for librarians

Workshops on Koha, digitisation, information literacy, searching and citation tools. Training

Joining takes four steps

  1. Download the CSTRDLP request proforma.
  2. Fill it in and email it to pl@pastic.gov.pk.
  3. PASTIC adds your library to PakCat and the consortium services.
  4. If you want Koha, PASTIC sets up your library on Koha Cloud.

Membership is free and open to universities, colleges, S&T and R&D organisations, and public and special libraries. Requirements and benefits
