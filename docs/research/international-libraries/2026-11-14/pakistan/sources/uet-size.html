Seminars / Lectures / Training Sessions

Research Enhancement Skills

🔎 1. Effective Use of Library and Online Scholarly Resources

  • 📚  Library resources and services
  • 🖥️ Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC) and library website navigation
  • 🌐 HEC Digital Library and UET subscribed databases
  • 🔍 Online searching techniques, keywords, Boolean operators, and advanced search strategies
  • ✅ Effective online searching techniques, keywords, Boolean operators, and advanced search strategies

🧠 2. Research Topic Selection, Critical Reading and Literature Review

  • 🎯 Identification and formulation of a research problem and topic
  • 🚧 Identifying research gaps through literature searching
  • 📖 Critical and technical reading of research articles and books
  • ⚖️ Evaluation, comparison, and synthesis of scholarly literature
  • 📝 Organizing and writing an effective literature review

✍️ 3. Research Proposal, Thesis and Research Paper Writing

  • 📐 Structure and components of a research proposal
  • 🎯 Formulation of research objectives, questions, and methodology
  • 📘 Structure and preparation of a thesis/dissertation
  • 🧾 Writing research papers, including abstract, introduction, methodology, results, discussion, and conclusion
  • 📊 Academic writing, formatting, presentation of tables and figures, editing, and proofreading

📑 4. Referencing, Academic Integrity and Research Publication

  • 📚 Citation and referencing styles, including APA and IEEE
  • 🗂️ Use of reference management tools - EndNote
  • 🔗 Proper paraphrasing, summarizing, and citation of sources
  • 🛡️ Plagiarism, academic integrity, and responsible use of information
  • 🔍 Use and interpretation of Turnitin similarity reports
  • 📰 Selecting appropriate journals based on scope, quality, indexing, and relevance
  • 🏅 HEC-recognized journals and major indexing databases
  • ⚠️ Identifying and avoiding predatory/deceptive/fake/substandard journals and publishers
  • ⚖️ Publication ethics, authorship, peer review, and manuscript submission
