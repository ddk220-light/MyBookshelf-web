                    Sahabzada Yaqub Khan (SYK) Library




Sahabzada Yaqub Khan (SYK) Library was founded in 1970. It is one of the well reputed
research libraries of the defence institutions. Its rich resources are a blend of print and
digital material with latest information services, which serve as a back-bone of all research
support activities at NDU. The SYK Library also maintains a fiction section that offers light
reading material to members and their families. Library subscribes a good quantity of
national and international journals and magazines to fulfil research and recreational
requirements       of      the       readers.         Library   can       be       accessed
http://syklibrary.ndu.edu.pk/libmax/opac/index.aspx

Digital Facilities
Digital Library is yet another endeavour by NDU Library to provide latest information
services to the members. Through this service books, journals, newspapers, articles and
online resources pertaining to various subjects are provided in electronic / digital format.
NDU publications like NDU Journal, Margalla Papers, Research Reports, Conference
Proceedings, ISSRA Papers, etc also form part of this collection.
