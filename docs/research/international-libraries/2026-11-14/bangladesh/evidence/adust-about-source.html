About Library

A Hub for Education & Research

Atish Dipankar University of Science & Technology (ADUST) Library is the Central Library of the university. It has been established since the founding of the university. Since its inception, the library has been meeting the needs of the university's teachers and students with its recent collections and sound management. As the university authorities give special importance to the library, along with other important issues, the library is making a special contribution in meeting the needs of the readers.

With special emphasis on recent developments advanced books, journals and other reading materials are collected every semester. University students and teachers can collect the texts they need from the library. With the support of the university authorities, ADUST Library is constantly working towards becoming a unique hub in the field of education and research.

Our Mission

The main objective of the library is to enhance educational resources and provide services to meet the research, teaching and learning needs of the university faculty members and students.

Our Vision

To build a reality-based knowledge resource center utilizing the latest technological developments of the 21st century and ensure the provision of new services and collections for the teaching, learning and research community.
