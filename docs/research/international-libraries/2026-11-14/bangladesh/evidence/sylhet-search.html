SAU Central Library

The Sylhet Agricultural University Central Library strives to provide accurate and timely information through high-quality resources and services, supporting the teaching, learning, and research needs of the university community. It envisions becoming a world-class Knowledge Resource Centre with innovative services and extensive collections in agriculture, enriched by the latest technological advancements of the 21st century. .
