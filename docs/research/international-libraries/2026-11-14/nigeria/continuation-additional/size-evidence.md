# Focused NISER and Ekiti collection-size pass

**Current physical collection totals remain unknown for both institutions.** NISER has strong evidence of a substantial historical specialist library, but the available numerical sources cannot establish its present national ranking.

| Institution | Measurement | Date and unit | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| NISER | About 18,000 | Bound volumes personally observed in January 1972; report published 1973 | Julian W. Witherell, Library of Congress, *Africana Acquisitions*, printed p. 5 / PDF p. 10. [Official report](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED089698.pdf#page=10). Historical, not current. |
| NISER | 6,000 | Volumes; January 16, 1969 | Nigerian Official Gazette No. 3, Vol. 56, printed p. 62 / PDF p. 26; also 38 reader seats. [Gazette](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/ng/1969/ng-government-gazette-dated-1969-01-16-no-3.pdf#page=26). Historical recruitment notice. |
| NISER | Over 900 | Journal subscriptions in a study collecting data May–June 2013 | [University of Ibadan repository copy](https://repository.ui.edu.ng/server/api/core/bitstreams/8d5d322f-05ae-43ec-bff4-324322b5f472/content), PDF p. 12, with 2015 publication material. This is not a physical book count. |
| Bishop Gabriel Oloniyo Public Digital Library, Ekiti | Unknown | Current physical collection | Official inauguration announcement and board page do not state volume totals. |

The 18,000 figure is NISER's; a nearby 25,000-volume figure in the same 1973 report describes another institute. Modern NISER India figures belong to a different institution and were excluded. The 13K+ resources on Nigeria's current ERAILS site describe OER, not its physical library.

For Ekiti, the press report of over 7,000 volumes describes Chief Femi Ajayi's library in Ire-Ekiti, built 2005 and later donated to the state. It is not the Oloniyo library inaugurated 2024. Historical registered-user counts for the state board, school/local-library responsibilities, and National Library of Nigeria's separate Ekiti branch were not converted into holdings or branch coverage.

No additional catalog searches were made during this focused pass. Raw PDFs, extracted text and HTTP request metadata are saved in `evidence/`; structured findings are in [size-evidence.json](size-evidence.json).
