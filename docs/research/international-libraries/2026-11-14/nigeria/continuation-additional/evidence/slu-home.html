Mission

To develop and implement strategies for effective provision of, and access to professional library and information services that support teaching, learning, research, and community engagement at Sule Lamido University.

Vision

To maintain a pivot position for all scholarly, academic, research and information related activities of a top-class university comparable with the best in Nigeria and beyond.

Philosophy

The philosophy of the University Library Services is the conviction that a modern university library should strive at all times to raise the intellectual capacity of its community through equitable access to information, innovation in service delivery, and responsive support for lifelong learning.
