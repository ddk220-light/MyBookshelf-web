Welcome to VUC Nigeria

Your Gateway to Nigerian Libraries

The Virtual Union Catalogue (VUC) is a revolutionary platform that brings together the catalogues of Nigerian libraries into a single, searchable database. Whether you're a student, researcher, or book enthusiast, VUC helps you locate books across multiple institutions without visiting each library physically.

Key Features: Search by title, author, subject, or institution. Certified users get access to call numbers, accession details, and PDF previews. Join our growing community of 4 libraries and 3 catalogue entries.
