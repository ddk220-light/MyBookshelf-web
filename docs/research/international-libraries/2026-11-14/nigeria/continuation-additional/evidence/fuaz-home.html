OPAC — Online Public Access Catalogue

Search the physical and digital holdings of the University Library.

Find Your Book

The OPAC system allows you to remotely search our extensive catalog before visiting the library in person. You can search by Title, Author, Keyword, or ISBN. Once you locate the material, note the Call Number and the physical floor location shown on the dashboard.

OPAC Search Engine

Powered by Koha Integrated Library System

Having Trouble Finding a Book?

If a book is listed as "Available" on OPAC but cannot be found on the shelf, it may be in use inside the reading room or awaiting re-shelving. Please consult the circulation desk on the ground floor.
