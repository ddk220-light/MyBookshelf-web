Welcome to the Adeleke University Library!

Adeleke University Libraries offers a wide range of educational materials and services. This library is an integral partner in the university’s efforts to achieve its core mission of teaching, research, and community engagement. With access to thousands of electronic resources available 24/7 through our online library catalog, virtual and physical locations offer resources for everyone.
