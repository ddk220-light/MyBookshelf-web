Welcome to the Taslim Elias Olawale Library Catalogue

The Nigerian Institute of Advanced Legal Studies was founded in March 17, 1979 as a centre for legal research and supervision for postgraduate research work. It was established as a result of the inadequate library facilities for legal research at the post graduate level. The Institute Library was conceived as a place where the texts and records on African law which were widely scattered outside Africa should be made available locally.The Library occupies a unique place in the Institute as it is pivotal to the effective discharge of the Institute functions. It provides basic research tools with emphasis on African law.
