Search Help
Library Catalogue Search lets you discover the full range of books, journals and other resources, both online and print, available via the library.

You can search the OPAC without logging in, click on the drop down menu besides the library catalogue box, and you  can use any of the following access points: Title, Author,Subject, ISBN, Series and Call number.

For more detailed search, click on the "advanced search".

The OPAC gives access to collection of students projects (undergraduate and post-graduate), text-books, e.books, reference sources, journals, journal articles, newspapers articles, government documents and so on.

Welcome to Tekena Tamuno Library, Redeemer's University.

Mission Statement

The mission of the Tekena Tamuno Library is to serve as a gateway to the latest information resources needed by the University Staff and students irrespective of location, to support research, teaching and service in a prompt, cost effective way.


Our Collection
  • Over 36,000 Volumes of Books
  • 1005 Journal titles / 20,018 Volumes
  • Over 15 Online and Networked Databases
  • Over 300 CD-ROMS
  • Coursework papers
  • Others
  • The Library also subscribes to Guardian, Punch, Vanguard, The Nation Newspapers and other information materials.
