Searching

Searching of catalogue is so easy, as easy as using google. Just type any keyword in the search box above and click on go.

For more exact searches, click on the arrow besides the library catalogue box, and you can use one of the following entry points:

  • Title

  • Author

  • Subject

  • ISBN

  • Series

  • Call Number

For more detailed search, click on the “advance search” link. You may now limit your searches using the item types in our collections, sort your search result by title, call number, author etc.
