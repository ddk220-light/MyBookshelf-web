How we help

Library services

Reader services

The Reader Services serves students and staff of the University, alongside researchers and the general public. All readers are welcome to consult Library materials in the reading room, during our regular opening hours.

Ask a Librarian

A Reference and Information Service aimed at guiding our Library patrons, and a means of getting and staying in touch with UAT Librarians for expert research help and recommendations for Books, Journals, Videos and other Library Information Resources.

Internet services

The UAT Library provides free internet services to all UAT community members (staff and students), accessed via our Public Access Computers located within the library complex.

Current Awareness Services

Informs our users about new acquisitions and collections in the UAT Library, through a variety of platforms like Billboards, Notices and our Social Media presence.

Reprographic services

Printing, Photocopying, Laminating, Scanning and Bindery services are offered in the Reprographic Services Unit of UAT Library.

Digital repository

The UAT Digital Repository is the home of the University's scholarly output: theses, research articles, journals and lectures, collected, preserved and shared openly.

Using the catalogue

Search tips

Advanced search →

  1. Search by title, author or subject

    Use complete or partial titles, the author's surname or full name, or keywords relating to your topic.

  2. Keep it short

    Use one or two important keywords rather than typing a complete sentence.

  3. Advanced search

    Combine title, author, subject, ISBN, publication year and material type for more precise results.

  4. Check availability

    Each record shows whether a copy is available, checked out or on hold before you visit the library.

  5. Renew online

    Log in to your account to renew eligible borrowed items.

Who can use the library

Eligibility for use

  • All students of the University of Africa, Toru-Orua
  • All Academic staff of the university
  • Senior Administrative Technical staff of the university recommended by the head of department
  • Other persons who need to make use of the library and have the approval of the University Librarian
