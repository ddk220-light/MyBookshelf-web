Institutional Heritage, Vision & Values

Nurturing Servant-Leaders Through Faith, Learning & Research.

The Laz Otti Memorial Library (LOML) and its 10 campus branch libraries serve as the intellectual nucleus of Babcock University, upholding an unyielding commitment to Christian education, missionary zeal, and infectious competence.

Our Vision

"A first-class university library nurturing servant-leaders for a better world."

Inspiring intellectual integrity, moral fortitude, and leadership across all academic pursuits.

Our Mission

"To support Christian education by providing quality resources and services that foster learning, research, leadership, and positive societal impact."

Equipping students and faculty with world-class informational resources rooted in Christian faith.

Heritage Timeline: 1959 to Date

Centrally Located Opposite BUCODEL

Originating in 1959 as Adekunle Alalade Library serving the Adventist College of West Africa (ACWA), the university library system has expanded into the centrally situated, purpose-built Laz Otti Memorial Library (LOML), located directly opposite BUCODEL, complemented by 10 specialized satellite branch libraries. Accommodating over 1,400 readers at full capacity (540 in main library, 761 in branches), the system is manned by 55 dedicated personnel (19 professional librarians and 33 support staff).

Reader Services, Registration & Circulation Desk

Decentralized Registration Across All Branches.

Membership is free for all registered Babcock students and staff. Registration is fully decentralized: you can register right at any of the 10 branch libraries or at the Laz Otti Main Library.

Mandatory Registration Requirements: Must present a valid Student or Staff I.D. card. Please note: Application number is strictly not acceptable. Students must be fully registered for the academic session to activate borrowing privileges.
Decentralized Register at any of 10 campus branches
2 to 4 Books Borrowing quota based on student level
Instant Clearance Graduation and semester clearance at LOML
2 Books Allowed
2 Weeks Duration

Undergraduate Borrowing Entitlement

Enrolled undergraduates receive 2 borrowing cards to allow them loan 2 books for 2 weeks. Provision is made for renewal after two weeks. Registration is decentralized across all host schools.

• Valid across all open stacks in Laz Otti Main Library & 10 branches • Renewal available after 2 weeks if no recall is logged • Free WiFi, Mendeley/Zotero citation support, and OPAC access included

Reprographic Services

Affordable low-cost student printing and photocopying: Printing @ ₦50 per page and Photocopying @ ₦40 per page, plus hardcover dissertation binding.

Reserve Book Service

Core course textbooks with limited copies or on heavy academic demand are placed on closed reserve behind the desk. Overnight and weekend loans are available upon card deposit.

Inter-Library Loans

Can't find a crucial book or article? Our Inter-Library Loan service sources monographs from libraries across the globe at no cost to you, supported by the University Librarian.

Complimentary Access

Except for minor nominal fees for external visitors (₦500/yr) and paper reprography, library resources, databases, WiFi, reference, and training courses are entirely free.
