University Librarian’s Welcome Address

Dear Students and Esteemed Colleagues,
It is with great pleasure and enthusiasm that I warmly welcome you all to our esteemed Library. As the University Librarian, I am delighted to have this opportunity to address you.
The university library serves as the heart of academic life, offering a vast array of resources and services to support your research, learning, and intellectual pursuits. Whether you are here to delve into scholarly texts, access online databases, seek assistance from our knowledgeable staff, or simply find a quiet space to study, our library is here to cater to your needs.
I encourage students to make the most of this resource-rich environment. Explore our collection of books, journals, and digital materials to deepen your understanding of your subjects and broaden your horizons. Our library staff is ready to assist you in navigating these resources effectively.
To our faculty and researchers, the library is your partner in academic excellence. We are committed to providing you with the resources necessary to support your teaching and research activities. Do not hesitate to reach out to us for personalized assistance or to discuss ways in which we can enhance our services to better serve your needs.
Beyond being a repository of knowledge, the library also serves as a hub for intellectual engagement and community building.

Prof. Abdulrahman Baba Abdulrazaq

University Librarian

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I find materials in the Library?

Use the Traditional Card catalogue or the Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC). Each contains materials held by the Library. If you cannot find what you need in the catalogue, please seek assistance at the Circulation Desk.

What should I do if I can’t find the book I need on the shelf?

Seek assistance at the Circulation Desk or from library personnel in the reading areas if you have difficulties locating a book on the shelf.

How can I get a book that is not available in the Library?

You may seek assistance of the Reference Librarian if the book you need is unavailable in the library. The library may request to borrow a copy of the item from another library via its Inter-Library Loan Service or purchase it.

How do I get login details for subscribed databases?

Visit the Library and request for login details from any of the e-librarians on duty.

Can I photocopy in the Library’s reading room?

Yes. The Library provides photocopying, printing and scanning services.
