About Ramat Library

About Us

Ramat Library is a modern library with a rich collection of print and electronic resources. We have 9 divisions, 13 branch libraries, an American Space section and also an International Institute of Islamic Thought section which provide access to information and knowledge to our users.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality library services, promote information literacy, and support the university's academic and research programs.

Special Information Centers

American Space

Located at the first floor of the Ramat Library main building. Provides resources on American culture, literature, and society, with access to free online databases and study materials. Free internet laptops for research and online programs.

International Institute of Islamic Thought (IIIT)

The premier international organization focusing on providing a platform for the unique perspective of Muslim thinkers, scholars and practitioners in the humanities and social sciences.

Library Services & Regulations

Research Services

Materials in the Research and Bibliographic Services Division may not be borrowed except with special permission of the University Librarian.

Serials Division

Current and backsets of periodicals may be borrowed by senior members of staff for overnight loan only.

E-Library Resources

The e-library has fifty (50) databases both open and subscribed. Access via our library website.

Library Penalties & Fines

Overdue Fines

  • Failure to return books
    ₦100 per day up to 7 days, then ₦500 per day
  • Failure to return recalled books
    ₦500 per day
  • Staff on study leave
    ₦500 per day per book until returned
  • Closed Access materials
    ₦200 per hour after 8:00 a.m due time
