ESUT Library

About the University Library

The Enugu State University of Science and Technology Library is the intellectual hub of the University, supporting teaching, learning, research, innovation, and community service through print, electronic, archival, and digital information resources.

History

Founded with the University in 1980.

The ESUT Library was established in 1980, the same year the University was founded, originally as Anambra State University of Science and Technology. It was created to serve as the intellectual hub of the institution, providing academic resources and information services for teaching, learning, and research.

Following the creation of Enugu State in 1991, the University became Enugu State University of Science and Technology. The Library also grew under this identity, expanding into a system of main and branch libraries that support the needs of faculties, campuses, staff, students, and researchers.

Today, the main library is located at the permanent site in Agbani, with branch libraries serving specialist academic communities including Medicine, Law, Engineering, Management Sciences, Environmental Sciences, Applied Natural Sciences, Education, and JUPEB students.

Vision

To be a dynamic, world class university library that promotes knowledge creation, research excellence, innovation, and lifelong learning through seamless access to information and services, thereby enhancing quality teaching, research, and community service for the benefit of the society.

Mission

To advance the University's mandate in science, management, and technology by providing timely access to current information resources and leveraging modern technologies to drive research, innovation, knowledge transfer, and human resource development.

Library Divisions

Services that connect resources with readers

Technical Services

Acquisition

Selects and obtains relevant books, journals, databases, and other learning materials that support the University’s teaching and research priorities.

Cataloguing and Classification

Describes, organizes, and classifies library materials so users can discover and locate them accurately through the catalogue and on the shelves.

Bindery

Repairs, binds, and preserves books, theses, journals, and other materials to extend their useful life and maintain the collection.

Readers Services

Circulation

Manages reader registration, borrowing, returns, renewals, reservations, and library clearance while helping users access lending collections.

Reference

Provides research guidance, answers information enquiries, supports literature searches, and helps users make effective use of reference resources.

Serials

Organizes and provides access to journals, newspapers, magazines, bulletins, reports, and other publications issued in successive parts.

Special Collections

Preserves and provides controlled access to rare, archival, institutional, and locally significant materials for learning and research.

Digital Library

Online access to knowledge and research

The Digital Library provides searchable access to electronic books, journals, theses, institutional research, databases, and other academic resources. It supports remote study, research discovery, document access, and digital preservation for students, staff, and researchers.

Locations and Branches

A library system built around faculty needs.

ESUT Library operates through the University Main Library at Agbani Campus and a network of branch and faculty libraries positioned to provide discipline-specific support.

Main Library: Central library and administrative hub at Agbani Campus.
Law Library: Legal studies, case law, statutes, law reports, and journals.
Medical Library: Parklane Campus resources for medicine and health sciences.
JUPEB Library: Foundation-level resources for direct-entry preparation.
Faculty Libraries: Engineering, Management Sciences, Environmental Sciences, Social Sciences, Natural Sciences, Education, Agricultural and Natural Resources, Applied Natural Sciences, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Allied Health Sciences, and Law.
