About ESUT E-Library

The intellectual hub for teaching, learning, and research at ESUT.

Established in 1980 with the University, ESUT Library collects, organizes, preserves, and provides seamless access to information resources that support education, research, innovation, and community service across print and electronic formats.

Read the library history
01

Main and branch libraries

The main library at Agbani works with faculty and campus branches to provide targeted academic support.

02

Digital access and OPAC

Modern ICT tools support Web OPAC, institutional repository access, e-resources, and remote discovery.

Mission, Vision & Values

Guided by access, accuracy, and academic integrity.

Guided by the University's motto, Technology for Service, the Library equips students and faculty with information skills, research support, and access to current academic resources.

Our Mission

To advance the University's mandate in science, management, and technology by providing timely access to current information resources and leveraging modern technologies to drive research, innovation, knowledge transfer, and human resource development.

Our Vision

To be a dynamic, world class university library that promotes knowledge creation, research excellence, innovation, and lifelong learning through seamless access to information and services.
