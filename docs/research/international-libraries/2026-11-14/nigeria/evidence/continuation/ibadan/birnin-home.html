Milestone · 22 September 2026
SSANU honours FUBK VC as Staff Welfare Ambassador
The SSANU FUBK Branch presents Prof. Muhammad Zaiyan Umar with a Staff Welfare Ambassador Award for implementing CONTTA in staff salaries and clearing outstanding arrears.
Campus · 30 September 2026
VC reaffirms commitment to addressing staff entitlements
Receiving delegations of SSANU, NASU and NAAT, Prof. MZ Umar says staff welfare remains a priority and commends the unions for sustaining industrial harmony.

FUBK to host Reproductive Right Society of Nigeria North-West copyright advocacy programme October 22
REPRONIG's one-day hybrid programme on the Nigerian Copyright Law will draw librarians, lecturers, authors, publishers and students from seven North-West states.
