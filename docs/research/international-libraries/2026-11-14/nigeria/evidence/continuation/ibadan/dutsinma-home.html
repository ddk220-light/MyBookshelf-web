Vice-Chancellor’s welcome

“Guided by the motto ‘Integrity and Service,’ FUDMA leverages modern information and communication technologies to enhance learning, research, and governance, while producing globally competitive graduates.”

Since its establishment in 2011, the University has grown into a vibrant academic community with over 30,000 students across 69 departments in seventeen faculties, providing quality education anchored on integrity, technology and relevance.

Prof. M.K. Othman
FNIAE, MNSE, MASABE · Vice-Chancellor
