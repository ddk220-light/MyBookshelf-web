Library Discovery Architecture

The NDA Library organises scholarly knowledge for global discoverability, citation visibility, and institutional web impact. Our cataloguing model supports researchers, cadets, faculty, and international partners with structured access to books, journals, theses, defence research, and digital academic resources.

The Nigerian Defence Academy Library is the premier academic library of the institution, providing comprehensive information resources and services to support the teaching, learning, and research activities of cadets, students, faculty, and staff. Established alongside the Academy, the Library has grown into a rich repository of knowledge spanning defence studies, military history, science, engineering, social sciences, and management.

Research Visibility and Global Reach

The Nigerian Defence Academy Library strengthens the Academy’s global academic presence by making its research, publications, theses, defence studies, and scholarly resources more visible and accessible to researchers, institutions, policy makers, and academic communities worldwide. Through well organised digital collections, searchable academic content, and internationally aligned indexing practices, the platform enhances the discoverability and citation of NDA research outputs across academic search engines and scholarly databases. This increased visibility not only showcases the intellectual contributions of faculty members, cadets, and researchers, but also reinforces the Academy’s reputation as a leading centre for defence education, innovation, and research excellence in Nigeria and beyond. 

Vision

To be a world-class academic library that supports excellence in teaching, learning, and research at the Nigerian Defence Academy and beyond.

Mission

To provide timely, relevant, and reliable information resources and services that support the academic, research, and professional development needs of the NDA community.

Institutional Library Story

The NDA Library was established in 1964 alongside the founding of the Nigerian Defence Academy. Since its inception, it has served as the intellectual hub of the institution, growing from a small collection of texts to a comprehensive academic library with tens of thousands of volumes, periodicals, and digital resources. Over the decades, the Library has continuously evolved to meet the changing information needs of its users, embracing technology and expanding its digital offerings to ensure that members of the NDA community have access to the best academic resources available.
