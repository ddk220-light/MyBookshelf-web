Welcome to Federal University Gusau

The Federal University Gusau (FUGUS) is a public, conventional, coeducational, research university located in Gusau, Zamfara State, Nigeria. We are delighted that you have chosen this University for your Undergraduate studies, with a reaffirmation of our commitment to high scholarly standard in accordance with international best practice.
