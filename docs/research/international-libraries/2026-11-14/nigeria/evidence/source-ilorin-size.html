Achievements

Since its establishment in 1975, the University of Ilorin Library has continuously evolved to enhance resource accessibility, technological integration, and support for academic and research excellence. Here are some notable achievements:

  • Established with a modest collection to support initial academic programs.
  • Adopted an automated cataloging system for efficient resource management.
  • Expanded the collection to over 100,000 volumes across various disciplines.
  • Introduced digital library services, providing access to e-books, e-journals, and online databases.
  • Established specialized libraries within faculties and departments.
  • Launched an institutional repository for the university’s scholarly output.
  • Implemented RFID technology for efficient inventory management and security.
  • Received recognition for outstanding support of research initiatives.
  • Expanded electronic resources with subscriptions to major academic databases and journals.
  • Introduced the Virtual Learning Commons for interactive online access to resources.
  • Launched user-centered services like personalized research consultations, online tutorials, and virtual help desks.
