Welcome to Kaduna State University Library Services

You are welcome to Kaduna State University Library Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC). Our library supports and supplements the teaching-learning program of the institution at all levels.

Searching of KASU OPAC is so easy, as easy as using Google. Just type any keyword in the search box above and click on go. For more exact searches, click on the arrow besides the library catalogue box, and you can use one of the following entry points:

  • Title
  • Author
  • Subject
  • ISBN
  • Series
  • and Call Number
For more detailed search, click on the “advance search” link. You may now limit your searches using the item types in our collections, sort your search result by title, call number, author etc.
