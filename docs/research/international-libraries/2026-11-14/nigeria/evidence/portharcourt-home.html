Divisions in donald ekong library

Administration

This Division coordinates all the administrative activities of the library.

Acquisition and Technical Division

This Division deals with the selection and acquisition of library information resources, and the maintenance of print-based information resources.

Information/Automation Services

This is the division that handles cataloguing of library resources and it is in charge of automation and digitization processes. The digitization processes involve the digitization of records, archival materials and the retrospective conversion of university administrative records.

Reader Services

This division is responsible for registering, charging and discharging library services to patrons. The other subunits in this division are: Circulation Unit, Reference Library Unit, Research Library Unit, Thesis Library Unit, Documents Library Unit, Archives Unit, Branch Libraries and Access Card Production Unit.

Bindery Unit

This unit is in charge of maintenance of books in the library

A Message from the University Librarian

Welcome to the Donald E.U. Ekong Library, the vibrant intellectual heart of the University of Port Harcourt. For generations, this Library has stood as a beacon of "Enlightenment and Self-Reliance," providing the foundation for UNIPORT's academic excellence. We are the custodians of a proud legacy begun by Professor Donald E.U. Ekong - a legacy we carry forward every day by empowering your research, learning, and innovation. Our mission is guided by core values of integrity, excellence, diversity, and collaboration. This means we are committed to curating world - class resources, providing expert support, and fostering inclusive spaces where every student and scholar can thrive. Your success is our purpose.

Today, we are building the library of the future. We are integrating artificial intelligence and data analytics to personalize your discovery of knowledge. We are transforming our physical spaces into adaptable hubs for quiet study, multimedia collaboration, and hands-on creation. 

Most importantly, we are deepening partnerships across campus, ensuring our librarians are active collaborators in your academic journey. The Donald E.U. Ekong Library is more than a collection of books; it is your catalyst for innovation, your gateway to global scholarship, and your intellectual home at UNIPORT. Explore our resources. Connect with our dedicated team. Let us support you not only in finding information, but also in creating new knowledge. We are here for you.

Warmly,

Prof. Helen U. Emasealu
B.A., MLS, Ph.D. (Ibadan) CLN, Associate, Mortenson Centre, Illinois
6th University Librarian
