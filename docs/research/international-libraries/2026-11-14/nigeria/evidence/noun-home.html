VISION

To provide first class information services that will enable and enhance all forms of research, the pursuit of knowledge and contribute to the development of the university and the society.

MISSION

To deliver world class library and information services to meet the needs of the local, national and international scholarly community, support the university's mission and contribute to the development of the society through teaching, learning and research.

CORE VALUES

Quality service, getting the basics right, continuous improvement, investing for the future, building and enhancing reputation, supporting diversity.
