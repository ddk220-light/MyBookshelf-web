    About Us

    Lagos State University was established in 1983 by the enabling law of Lagos State with the vision of pursuing the advancement of learning and academic excellence. The university has been unrelenting in the pursuit of its mission of becoming a citadel of learning, a community with the trademark of excellence in teaching, research and service to humanity.
