Gateway to Infinite Knowledge

Endless Learning

Our Legacy and Commitment:

Established in 1965, the University Library stands as a testament to our enduring commitment to education, research, and intellectual exploration. With a vast collection exceeding 500,000 volumes and access to over 30,000 periodicals and electronic resources, we empower students, faculty, and staff to reach new heights of scholarly achievement.

A Hub for Learning and Discovery:

At the heart of UNILAG’s academic ecosystem, our library is more than just a repository of books—it’s a dynamic hub that fosters learning, research, and creativity. Our mission is clear: to support teaching, nurture a culture of information literacy, and provide a welcoming environment conducive to intellectual growth.

What Sets Us Apart:

  1. Diverse Collections: Explore a treasure trove of print and digital resources spanning all disciplines, ensuring you have access to the latest research and academic insights.
  2. Specialist Libraries: Tailored collections for each Faculty ensure easy access to materials relevant to your academic pursuits.
  3. Cutting-Edge Technology: Embrace the digital age with seamless access to online databases, e-journals, and e-books, available anytime, anywhere.
  4. Expert Guidance: Our team of dedicated librarians is on hand to assist with research strategies, resource location, and citation support, empowering you to excel in your studies.
  5. Modern Facilities: From state-of-the-art computer labs to comfortable study areas and printing services, we provide the tools and spaces you need for productive learning.

 

Legal Depository

Under the Publications Law, (Cap.107, of the Laws of the Lagos State of Nigeria. 1973), the Library became a legal depository for two copies of all books published in Lagos State. The library is also a depository for the publications of United Nations Economic Commission for Africa (E.C.A); General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade, (GATT); International Court of Justice (ICJ) and orders for selected publications of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) World Health Organization (WHO) and Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)

 

Getting Started:

Whether you’re a new student eager to explore our resources or a seasoned researcher seeking specialized support, the University of Lagos Library is here for you. Dive into our online catalog, access digital resources, attend orientation sessions, or reach out to our friendly staff for personalized assistance.

Opening Hours:

  • During Term:
    • Monday to Saturday                          –        8.00am – 10.00pm
    • Sunday                                             –        2.00pm – 8.00pm
  • During Vacation
    • Monday to Saturday                          –        8.00am – 6.00pm

 

Contact Us:

Visit us in person, explore our website at https://library.unilag.edu.ng/, or connect via email at library@unilag.edu.ng. Your journey to knowledge begins here at the University of Lagos Library.
