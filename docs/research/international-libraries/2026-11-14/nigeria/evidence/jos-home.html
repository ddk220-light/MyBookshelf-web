Welcome to Our Library Catalog

 Your gateway to books, e-resources, and more.

This is your space to access knowledge. It is also a space to interact with staff and students. Library staff have put in much effort to ensure that you have easy and direct access to information resources. You have the Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC) and links to qualitative resources in the library and elsewhere for your academic pursuits.

Here, you can;
✓ Browse our collection of books, journals, and digital resources
✓ Check due dates and renew items online
✓ Place holds on unavailable items
✓ Save favorite titles with your personal account

To enjoy the full services, log on to your account. If you are not registered, please do so. If you have forgotten your password call the library at 08026682849 or email libsys@unijos.edu.ng to get a username and a password to log in, Visit the library desk. Do make sure you have a valid email address in your record so that you can receive library notices. Enjoy!
