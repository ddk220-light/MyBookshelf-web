Donate

Library Opening Hours

The Central Library is open during official working days to serve students and academic staff.

Working Days: Sunday – Thursday Opening
Time: 8:30 AM – 7:30 PM
Closed: Friday and Saturday

The library may extend its opening hours during examination periods and important academic seasons. Any changes to the schedule will be officially announced through the library website and university communication channels. Users are encouraged to visit the library during opening hours and follow the library regulations to ensure a productive environment for all.

About the Library

The Central Library at the University of Duhok is the main library that serves all colleges, departments, and research centers within the university. It provides a comprehensive collection of academic and educational materials that support undergraduate and postgraduate study, academic teaching, and scientific research.

The library contains a diverse range of printed collections, including textbooks, reference books, academic publications, journals, newspapers, and research reports. In addition, it maintains a valuable archive of theses and dissertations produced by university students, which contribute significantly to academic development and research progress.

Vision

The vision of the Central Library is to become a leading academic library in the Kurdistan Region and Iraq by providing advanced information services, modern academic facilities, and innovative digital resources that support excellence in teaching, learning, and research.

Mission

The mission of the Central Library is to support the academic community of the University of Duhok by ensuring access to high-quality information resources, professional library services, and a modern learning environment.
