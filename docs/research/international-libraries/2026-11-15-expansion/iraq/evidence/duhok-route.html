Library Catalog System

The Central Library is committed to supporting modern research and learning by providing access to digital academic resources. Through its e-library services, users can access electronic materials, academic databases, and online research tools that support education and scientific research.

Digital resources are essential for postgraduate students, researchers, and academic staff who require quick access to global scientific publications. The Central Library continuously works to expand its digital services and strengthen access to international academic platforms.

Users may access these resources through the library portal, university network, or through approved login systems depending on the resource provider.
