# Angola — library marketplace and catalog feasibility

Research date: 15 November 2026 UTC. **3 institutions/networks with successful book searches; 3 selected targets; 0 reserves.** Fourteen substantial priorities and further alternatives were researched. The source study and bounded catalog sweep are complete; the ten-selected goal has a **shortfall of seven**. Selection confirms an observed source query, not a tested MyBookshelf integration.

Start with **Portuguese Koha at Biblioteca Nacional de Angola and Universidade Lusíada de Angola**. Both returned exact-title records with holdings. **ReMA** is the strongest evidenced shared public-network opportunity: its custom POST catalog returned a Luanda holding, but malformed metadata and unknown circulation need explicit treatment. Each network counts once.

## Market, access and shared coverage

[Biblioteca Nacional de Angola](https://www.bibliotecanacional.ao/missao/) combines legal deposit, national bibliography, public-library coordination and a collective-catalog mandate. Only its own holding was verified; these responsibilities do not prove a live countrywide holdings union. The [Municipal Library of Luanda](https://www.opais.ao/cultura/biblioteca-municipal-de-luanda-aposta-na-projeccao-de-obras-em-braile-para-leitores-invisuais/) remains a substantial public target without an established public query route.

[ReMA](https://www.mediatecas.gov.ao/Historial-da-ReMA) provides regional multimedia-library coverage through an officially linked [shared OPAC](https://remagest.gov.ao/catalogo/). Its history lists eight opened mediatecas, current navigation ten names, and catalog sixteen selector entries including proximity/administrative entries. These are not additive or proof of sixteen searchable institutions. The website records 2024 provincial management transfers, so governance needs reconciliation. Only branch filter `mediateca=14` and returned **Mediateca de Luanda /Luanda** were verified.

Central and faculty collections at UAN, UCAN, Lusíada, UMN, UKB, Metodista and Piaget remain significant. [UMN](https://umn.edu.ao/umn/index.php/umn/informacao-util/bibliotecas) names five faculty/institute libraries under central coordination; external ebook lists do not establish their physical holdings. [UAN's 2023 ReMA visit](https://www.mediatecas.gov.ao/public/noticia/BIBLIOTECA%2BCENTRAL%2BDA%2BUNIVERSIDADE%2BAGOSTINHO%2BNETO%2BE%2BREDE%2BDE%2BMEDIATECAS%2BDE%2BANGOLA%2BBUSCAM%2BPARCERIA) sought cooperation, without proving a completed union.

Reader access differs: [ISAF](https://isaf.co.ao/biblioteca/) describes wider-community access; [ISA](https://isa.ao/investigacao/biblioteca) separates physical Koha from institution-credential digital content; [ISPTEC's PDI](https://www.isptec.co.ao/public/assets/uploads/docs/PDI_ISPTEC_2025.pdf) describes academic-community lending. [ReMA's undated policy](https://www.mediatecas.gov.ao/Pol%C3%ADticas-de-utilizacao) describes ID, a personal card and one-time 2,000-kwanza issuance charge; the current fee was not independently confirmed. Anonymous lookup does not establish admission, lending eligibility or future availability.

## Blockers and meaningful alternatives

- **ISA:** [robots](evidence/isa-robots.html) explicitly excludes `/cgi-bin/koha/opac-search.pl`; Allow for detail does not override that. No search or alternate-route evasion.
- **Sol Nascente:** [robots](evidence/ispsn-robots.html) excludes `/busca/` despite broader Allow /. The longest path wins. No query; separate institutional website challenge also not bypassed.
- **Metodista:** two title queries (`Redes de Computadores`, `Protecção Jurídica do Software`) and ISBN `9789727227815` each returned zero. Yet [direct record 250](evidence/uma-detail.json) shows REDES DE COMPUTADORES, JOSE GOUVEIA E ALBERTO MAGALHAES, Lisboa FCA 2009, and an available copy. This is **unproductive search, not absence**, and direct-record lookup alone does not meet the query criterion.
- **ISPTEC:** official link misspells `bibloteca`. An independently documented correctly spelled HTTP route opened; [robots](evidence/isptec-historical-http-robots.json) allowed public paths. Public frontend JavaScript exposes anonymous `/api/consulta`; one `Fundamentos de física` query returned [HTTP 500 PDOException / Database error](evidence/isptec-query.json). No retry, private/internal route or TLS bypass.
- **ISAF:** public-path robots was followed by [HTTP 403 JavaScript/cookies challenge](evidence/isaf-home.json). Stopped without a title query.
- **Assembleia Nacional:** officially linked Koha title query returned [HTTP 530 Origin DNS error 1016](evidence/parliament-query.json) after allowed-path robots. No holding and no retry.
- **Tribunal Constitucional:** [HTTP 200 catalog](evidence/tribunal-catalog.json) is a static title/author table without an observed query form, individual item contract or circulation. Retained as a specialist research priority.
- **UAN, UCAN, UMN and Municipal Luanda:** substantial collections, no usable anonymous local holdings route established. Repositories, portals and external ebooks are not substituted.
- **Piaget:** [official archive](evidence/piaget-archive.json) returned 404 after robots 404. Piagest mentions do not establish an LMS or query contract.
- **UKB Medicine:** [official page](https://www.fmukb.edu.ao/library/) features books/status words without a submitted search, stable record/edition identity or item evidence.
- **ISPEKA and UJES:** campus-only Biblivre cannot support public lookup; [UJES resource links](https://ujes.ao/investigacao/publicacao) are not local physical holdings.
- **Instituto Guimarães Rosa, Angola:** [international catalog](https://igr.opac.ar/pergamo/opac.php?l=PT) has an Angola selector, without verified filtered-book/Angolan-holding proof. **INSUTEC's** virtual-library claims were not converted to physical collection size.

## Transport incident and evidence limits

At about 08:24 UTC, [unrelated controls](evidence/execution-network-controls.json) returned identical proxy upstream-connect 503 bodies. BNA's initial [08:30:41 robots request](evidence/bna-robots.json) matched, so no query followed while policy was unresolved. [Client comparisons](evidence/execution-client-comparison.json) then showed partial recovery, and several independent catalogs returned responses. One fresh BNA robots check at 08:37:06 returned ordinary 404, followed by a successful title query. Both observations remain. This was a **research transport incident**, not an Angola-wide service failure.

The [source register](evidence/source-register.json), [priority register](evidence/major-priority-register.json), [request ledger](evidence/request-ledger.json) and [structured findings](findings.json) distinguish indexed/cached source discovery from exact live HTTP evidence. Responses are bounded. Search failure never establishes not held.

The seven-target shortfall follows research of major institutions and meaningful alternatives. No national top-ten ranking or LMS share was established. No authentication, challenge, private-network or robots bypass, saved account state, outreach, holds, app changes or deployment occurred. All selected entries have `live_app_tested: false`.
