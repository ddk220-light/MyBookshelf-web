# Thailand — library expansion research, 15 November 2026

**12 documented institution observations: 10 selected, 2 reserves, from three actual title searches against one UC-TAL public union API.** These are institution-specific book listings, not ten independently working catalogs or live circulation checks. UC-TAL is not counted as an additional library. The selected sample is academic; the National Library, Bangkok public libraries and Chiang Mai remain important priorities despite access or verification gaps.

The best first integration is a dedicated **UC-TAL/WALAI union adapter** that answers “listed at this institution” and preserves the union record, local member and edition. It must not label this as “available to borrow.” The public API resolved books across heterogeneous local systems, including catalogs whose own automated search is restricted. Kasetsart’s subsequent item relay returned HTTP500, and several returned local links are visibly older than current library systems.

## Market and network assessment

Thailand combines national cultural/deposit services, municipal/public libraries and large university networks. The National Library reported **5,368,998 physical items for calendar 2023**, not 5.37 million books. Its provincial directory describes separate sites including Suphan Buri, Songkhla, Chon Buri and Nakhon Phanom, with their own service arrangements. The selected ten do not substitute for this public-service opportunity. [2024 country report, covering 2023](https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/cdnlao/meetings/pdf/2024%20thailand-%20report.pdf); [official regional directory](https://www.nlt.go.th/library?id=25).

Bangkok’s policy describes **36 public libraries and 139 book houses**, and plans easier cross-library lending plus at least 10,000 ebooks. Those are a network proxy and a future ebook target, not a verified physical catalog total. The portal’s robots request failed with HTTP503; no catalog query followed. A separate Bangkok Art and Culture Centre ELIB catalog was discovered but not tested or conflated with the BMA network. [BMA policy](https://policy.bangkok.go.th/watch/apolicies261/view?code=P169&id=169).

UC-TAL is the physical/mixed-material **bibliographic union** opportunity. A Silpakorn institutional guide dated April/August 2023 reports over 100 institutions and over 2.2 million bibliographic records and explains that each result identifies the holding library. These historical counts are not a current complete holdings census. ThaiLIS also supports TDC digital/full-text resources; journal indexes, repositories, ebooks and the union are different products. PULINET is the provincial **university** network, not a municipal public-library system. [UC-TAL guide](https://snc.lib.su.ac.th/kmblog/?p=10510); [ThaiLIS/PULINET explanation](https://snc.lib.su.ac.th/snclibblog/?p=51468).

The strongest scoped platform evidence is mixed:

- A **1 August 2015** institutional overview lists **WALAI AutoLib 68, ALIST 27 and LM-SomSook 2 adopting organizations**. This shows a substantial Thai-developed ecosystem, but is old and is not national market share. [Historical overview](https://snc.lib.su.ac.th/snclibblog/?p=49920).
- **WALAI/UC-TAL:** the live public frontend specifies a WALAI-hosted `/ucopacapi/v1` service. Burapha has an institutional WALAI AutoLib Ultimate announcement. Silpakorn now exposes `suopac.lib.su.ac.th`; its union link still points to an older route. [Burapha](https://www.lib.buu.ac.th/walai-autolib-ultimate-2/); [Silpakorn migration notice](https://snc.lib.su.ac.th/kmblog/?p=16363).
- **ALIST:** PSU’s academic-year 2022 QA identifies ALIST for Khunying Long and reports 169,042 printed information-resource titles for that center. Its current OPAC has a React frontend/public JSON API plus older `opac2` links. The one bounded `Harry Potter` probe returned five videorecordings, so it is not a successful book test. Sakon Nakhon’s announcement and returned ALIST deep link provide a separate institutional example. [PSU QA](https://clib.psu.ac.th/images/PDF_file/AUN_QA/AUN-QA_Information_Academic_Year_2022.pdf); [Sakon Nakhon](https://arit.snru.ac.th/topics/2968).
- **Innovative and Koha:** Kasetsart’s fetched homepage expressly credits WebPAC PRO/Innovative; its `/search` path is disallowed. Thammasat and Rambhai Barni union mappings return specific Koha record URLs. This is reuse potential, not a local adapter test.
- **Alma/Primo:** Mahidol’s March 2025 report explicitly describes Sierra-to-Alma migration training for **38 libraries**; its current site links Primo. The older faculty facilities page describes 25 branches and mixed holdings, so those counts are not silently combined. National Library’s official form points to Primo NDE; robots disallows `/nde/search`, `/discovery/search` and `/primaws`. [Mahidol migration](https://www.li.mahidol.ac.th/news/21mar2025-2).
- **ULibM:** a 2014 NSTDA knowledge-sharing article documents Thai development and standards-based interoperability; specialist and education deployments were discovered. No national adoption share or ULibM book-query success is claimed. [ULibM](https://www.thailibrary.in.th/2014/08/04/ulibm/).

## Reader access and integration constraints

The UC-TAL guide requires registration and current academic-library membership for ILL. Public search is not automatic visitor admission or borrowing permission. Chula’s official prospectus explicitly includes the public in its audience; KMUTNB’s university page includes general-public resource access; Ubon reports actual outsider use. Mahidol’s subscribed electronic resources require its account. Individual lending limits, fees and circulation eligibility were not tested. National Library’s “The Reader” postal program is a distinct selected-book lending service; it does not make every national-library item borrowable.

Implement the union as a dedicated source with `union bibID + member locationID + local record ID`, preserving Thai titles, translator/edition identity, material type and returned call numbers. Never infer availability from an institution association or an empty serial-holding array. `Holding/GetHolding` returned empty arrays for five books despite named member associations; this is not absence. The only attempted live item relay, Kasetsart `GetItemByLocation`, returned HTTP500. [failed item response](evidence/uctal-ku-items.html).

This evidence supports an institution-listed discovery prototype, with a separate future review of ongoing reuse, freshness and supported circulation access. It does not establish production-ready connectivity. Existing Koha code might help independently allowed installations, but the verified success here is the new union family. Do not propagate stale Mahidol/Silpakorn legacy URLs as current verified destinations.

## Evidence and limits

[findings.json](findings.json) is the common-schema inventory, including exact methods/payloads/timestamps, member mappings, local-link caveats, size units/dates and reader-access notes. Bounded response bodies and request metadata are under [evidence](evidence/). All search responses were manually inspected. The downloaded public frontend bundle has one unused application key redacted, with a retention note; no token response or Authorization value is saved.

There is no national LMS census, largest-ten ranking, full-university coverage guarantee or live-app test. The sample’s ten selected institutions rest on one public union endpoint and three title queries; live circulation and regional/municipal physical catalogs remain open work. No app configuration, accounts, holds, messages, outreach or deployments changed.
