      How it works

      • Ask naturally

        Type a topic, a method or a title, for example "NaOH used in food processing", "ANOVA method in Financial Analysis", "Social media usage in teenage".

      • Narrow with a keyword

        Add “book” or “research” (หนังสือ, งานวิจัย) to search only the catalog or only articles and theses.

      • Get the results

        AI-powered search, summaries, and suggestions from KU Library and research articles by leading publishers.

      • Open the sources

        Use links to explore the catalog record or the full-text papers and resources.

      • Check what matters

        AI can rarely make mistakes. Always confirm important details in the original source.
