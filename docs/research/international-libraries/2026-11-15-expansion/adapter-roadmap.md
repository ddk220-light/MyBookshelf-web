# Adapter roadmap for the additional-country research

Reviewed 15 November 2026 against the 25 country dossiers, the [earlier adapter audit](../2026-11-14/adapter-readiness.md), and current provider code. Country recovery work may change totals; this roadmap names representative evidence-backed candidates rather than freezing a coverage count. **No new country adapter was implemented, tested in the live app or deployed by this review.**

Start with Koha compatibility, then extend PMB through explicit deployment profiles. Build new families where multiple observed deployments justify the work. Treat union-catalog discovery as a separate deliverable from live copy availability.

## What existing code actually supports

**Koha offers the lowest incremental engineering effort.** Existing code covers search, record resolution, branch scoping and availability, but expects a root-mounted `/cgi-bin/koha/` OPAC. Its richer search parser requires English result headings and particular HTML markers; a Portuguese, French, Arabic or customized page can return books while failing that parser. Single-result redirects to a detail record are a legitimate response, not an empty search.

Before broadening Koha, fix the previously demonstrated **“Room Use Only” Notes-column loss** and replay restricted/reference, checked-out, lost, in-transit and unknown statuses. Preserve original labels alongside conservative normalization. Native-script input, Unicode display and legacy character decoding need explicit fixtures; an English query against a multilingual library does not verify its other languages. [Koha implementation](../../../../server/services/providers/koha.js).

**PMB and VuFind are not arbitrary-host configuration switches.** Current PMB support is pinned to `mediatheques-afinde.org` and known scopes; VuFind accepts only KALNET and Somaiya. Reuse parsing/session patterns while adding reviewed deployment profiles and tests. UNS's “Library System Unavailable” and VUC's maintenance response mean unknown item status, not all copies checked out. Mexico's ADABI collections are partial heritage discovery, not whole-library circulation. [PMB configuration](../../../../server/services/providers/discoveryCommunity.js), [PMB implementation](../../../../server/services/providers/communityPmb.js), [VuFind settings](../../../../server/services/providers/vufind.js).

The [registry](../../../../server/services/providers/index.js) has no named SophiA, Pergamum, Pérgamo, UC-TAL, CCdz, SYNGEB, KIPOS or Vebrary provider. `discovery-academic` also accepts only its approved configurations; it is not a generic custom-catalog escape hatch. A Koha-backed custom frontend, such as Bugema or lib.iq, needs its own observed request/response contract. South African SLIMS is distinct from Indonesian Senayan SLiMS; Argentine Pérgamo is distinct from Brazilian Pergamum.

Other custom systems remain per-site projects until a shared contract is demonstrated.

## Shared acceptance conditions

1. **Identity:** maintain deployment → institution → branch → bibliographic record → item. Preserve source record IDs, edition/format, authors, publication data and valid record-owned ISBNs. Never substitute a cover-image ISBN, merge editions by title, strip CCdz leading zeros, or turn a temporary session URL into a permanent identifier. lib.iq's shared record contains conflicting institution-level years; resolve the local edition before attaching its union ISBN to a copy.
2. **Evidence and status:** prove search input, actual returned book, record resolution and holding scope independently. Empty bodies, failed details, timeouts and unavailable backends mean “could not check.” Broad matches are not exact-title evidence. Bibliographic discovery, a holding institution, a physical stock count and current circulation are different outputs.
3. **Reader access:** keep onsite-only, membership, visitor admission and borrowing eligibility separate from item status. Public catalog access does not establish public borrowing. National heritage collections, academic libraries and municipal lending networks require different descriptions.
4. **Transport:** retain the current lookup wrapper's robots checks, crawl delays, host budgets, cancellation and response limits. Review each origin, redirect and mount path; do not generalize TLS exceptions or fall back automatically to HTTP. Current reviewed HTTP support is Koha-specific, so non-Koha HTTP candidates need transport/public-link design work. Fresh anonymous cookies/tokens stay in memory; no saved nonce or patron credential becomes configuration.
5. **Release evidence:** add meaningful fixture cases for successful, restricted, empty, malformed, wrong-institution and failed responses, then bounded permitted live checks through the app layer. The registry currently requires `availability` even to expose catalog methods; do not advertise supported circulation merely to satisfy that gate. Explicitly model unknown availability for discovery-only providers and validate the registration contract before enabling directory validation flags.

## Opportunities that remain recovery work

Network membership does not establish an immediately usable connector. Colombian public networks, Egypt's EULC, Kenya KNLS, Tanzania TLSB, Argentina SIU BDU and Türkiye KYGM/TO-KAT remain institution/operator-interface investigations to varying degrees. Recovery checks proved a Luanda-attributed book through Angolan ReMA's custom POST catalog, ENSEA PMB and IFCI Bibliosoft in Côte d'Ivoire, and UEM's legacy iAH/ISIS route alongside UJC Koha in Mozambique. Those are concrete additional adapter candidates, with the scope and metadata limitations in their dossiers; they do not establish nationwide coverage or live circulation. Ethiopia's Abrehot/African Union custom catalogs and Ghana's digital OPDS route require distinct scopes; a digital feed must not become a physical-holdings claim.

Defer interactive Yordam rollout at Türkiye's Millet Library while its observed **300-second crawl delay** remains incompatible with ordinary on-demand lookup. Do not select robots-restricted UAAAN in Mexico or protected Primo/Janium routes for implementation merely because an earlier query succeeded. Guanajuato is a different origin with saved404 robots and remains a candidate; it must not inherit UAAAN's restriction. Restricted cases need a permitted interface or an explicitly different access arrangement; this review sends no outreach and makes no production changes.
