## Scale, market and access assessment

The reports generally preserve source dates, units and scopes. Physical volumes, distinct titles, mixed print/digital materials, catalog records, seats and network units are not summed into a national total. Examples include UNICAMP's mixed historical total, UFPR's component collection, Mahidol's mixed resources, ULPGL's ebook-inclusive count, UNS's mixed discovery facets and Vietnam's merged or legacy public-library identities. National priorities remain visible even when a smaller accessible catalog was found.

Selection remains a research judgment, not a uniform collection-size threshold. **Twenty-eight of 105 selected observations lack a numeric collection value** and use explicit institutional scope proxies; additional numeric values measure seats or network extent. Small national special libraries and university networks are sometimes selected on mandate or reach, while smaller specialist collections remain reserves elsewhere. This is defensible under the stated opportunity-screen framing. The final synthesis does not relabel these as the largest libraries or quantitatively comparable targets.

LMS claims distinguish direct implementation evidence, dated studies, vendor footprints and this feasibility-selected sample. The Colombian historical study's overlapping vendor categories, Sri Lanka's scoped university sample and Thailand's 2015 adopter counts are not promoted to current national shares. Repositories, electronic subscriptions, journal unions, coordination bodies and branch directories are separated from institution-attributed physical-book discovery.

Reader eligibility is generally stated separately from catalog access. Room-use, reference, reserve, not-for-loan, in-transit, on-loan and damaged-book meanings are preserved. Particularly useful counterexamples are NVIC's no-borrowing policy despite an `Available` item, Argentina UNS's backend failure rather than an unavailable copy, Vietnam's unloaded zero placeholders and nightly snapshot, and South Africa's unresolved live-status fields. No item snapshot establishes visitor entitlement or a reuse licence.

## Request and policy assessment

The saved successful POST flows retain semantic payloads and fresh-form/session recipes. Browser state is not needed to understand the intended requests. Modern SophiA, PMB, CCdz and other form-based routes require their documented sequence; historical nonces and set IDs are not replay credentials. Missing durable record URLs are left unresolved instead of inventing GET links.

The final shared policy audit covers **277 declared requests/links with no unacknowledged restriction** and correctly refuses to apply one origin's robots file to another. Thailand's union-supplied local links consequently remain unchecked; Bugema's separately captured native-detail policy is used for that distinct origin. A successful query or a 404 robots response is not continuing permission to crawl or reuse records.

The policy audit covers declared query and linked-record paths, not every source request, redirect, AJAX stage, timing interval or future use. It must not be described as a blanket compliance certificate. Two disclosed timing deviations remain part of the research record: the unsuccessful Congo UCC chain omitted Mikanda's ten-second delay, and the initial Turkish Millet homepage preceded enforcement of its newly discovered 300-second delay. The subsequent successful UPC and Millet title sequences respect the documented intervals; independently checked AU Library and CABA sequences also respect their stated delays. UAAAN's incorrect initial robots interpretation remains excluded from selection.

No new live verification was performed for this review. Broad proxy failures around 08:21 UTC are an execution limitation; they neither negate earlier successes nor establish countrywide catalog downtime or absent holdings. The final package incorporates the later independently successful country requests and preserves unsuccessful cases without inferring absence. **Accept as a qualified research package with a clearly stated 145-target shortfall; not as clearance for production integration, general public borrowing or live circulation.**
