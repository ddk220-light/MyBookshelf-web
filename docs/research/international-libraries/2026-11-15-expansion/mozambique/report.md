# Mozambique library expansion research

**4 observed book-search successes: 2 selected, 2 reserves; selected shortfall 8.** UEM and UJC are selected; ISUTC is a reserve because scale is unquantified, and Zambeze is a reserve because its physical/digital support semantics are unresolved. Market/source research is complete. Remaining transport failures and missing endpoints are institution-specific; no countrywide infeasibility is claimed.

**Recommendation:** investigate institution-specific Portuguese Koha integrations first, led by UJC and the BNM/UNISCED candidates, then locate UCM's public deployments. Add a separate connector investigation for the now-verified UEM iAH/ISIS route. The evidence supports repeatable adapter-family opportunities, not a national LMS market share or ten production-ready integrations.

## Marketplace and access

Mozambique has national/public libraries, government documentation services and university networks with different eligibility rules. BNM's national mandate includes legal deposit and coordination of public libraries; no shared national physical-book catalog was verified. INE's *Anuário Estatístico 2024*, page 49, reports **141 libraries** in its monthly-library-survey series, including 28 in Maputo City and 19 each in Sofala and Tete. This is a scoped statistical series, not an exhaustive LMS census or a count of separately integrable institutions. [BNM mandate](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/mz/2024/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2024-05-10-no-91.pdf) · [INE table](https://www.ine.gov.mz/documents/20119/366649/ANUARIO%20ESTAT%C3%8DSTICO_%202024.pdf/b138faa5-ff0f-839c-6bd8-0c234c1b7a9b?download=true&t=1760443453700&version=1.0)

University networks offer more concentrated integration opportunities. UEM's official documentation page reports 13 libraries including Biblioteca Central Brazão Mazula; its documentation service coordinates acquisitions, cataloguing and a collective catalog. UCM likewise describes 13 libraries, but its page contains historical rollout language, so the current configuration needs checking. Each network is one institutional target here. [UEM](https://uem.mz/direccao-dos-servicos-de-documentacao/) · [UCM](https://ucm.ac.mz/bibliotecas/)

Public discovery and reader access are different. UP-Maputo explicitly welcomes the public. UJC permits interested external readers with identification and a control sheet, but reserves home loans for internal users. UCM students can use its libraries with an updated student card; visitors must arrange access with the relevant faculty. CEDIMO publishes reader-card requirements and separate photo-ID consultation for legislation/newspapers. These rules cannot be generalized across Mozambique. [UP](https://up.ac.mz/bibliotecas) · [UJC regulation](https://biblioteca.ujc.ac.mz/assets/regulamento-bibliotecas.pdf) · [UCM](https://ucm.ac.mz/bibliotecas/) · [CEDIMO](https://www.cedimo.gov.mz/index.php/biblioteca-mediateca)

## Additional alternatives and next verification

UNISCED is a useful additional Koha candidate; its official learning portal advertises 18k+ students, but that undated figure does not quantify the library. ISUTC now has an actual verified query and an official service page allowing internal/external students; collection scale remains unknown, so it stays a reserve. UniAC advertises 1,015 catalog books and expressly warns that availability may not be current: it belongs among smaller pilots until stronger scale evidence is available. Banco de Moçambique's November 2024 dissemination policy confirms a research-library access channel, but no public catalog was found. [UNISCED](https://salavirtual.unisced.edu.mz/?lang=pt&redirect=0) · [ISUTC](https://www.isutc.ac.mz/?p=3302) · [UniAC](https://uniac.ac.mz/biblioteca/) · [Banco de Moçambique policy](https://bancomoc.mz/media/d2odloai/pol%C3%ADtica-de-dissemina%C3%A7%C3%A3o-e-revis%C3%B5es-das-estat%C3%ADsticas-do-banco-de-mo%C3%A7ambique.pdf)

Resume with one fresh robots request per genuinely reachable official origin. Where allowed, inspect the public form, submit one specific title, retain bounded raw search/detail responses and HTTP metadata, and record institution-specific holdings. Do not count branch selectors, generic repository matches, purchase requests or whole-network totals as separate libraries.

## Limits and retained evidence

This dossier contains source research for ten major priorities and useful additional candidates, with **two selected live targets, two qualified reserves and eight selected targets still missing**. Size evidence is non-comparable: BNM is historical, Lúrio mixes physical/electronic resources, UJC uses a 2021 baseline, and several institutions use explicit scale proxies.

Files named `web-discovery-*.json` retain bounded web-tool outputs and saved timestamps, not raw origin HTML or exact live catalog HTTP metadata. The `ujc-*`, `uem-*`, `isutc-*` and `zambeze-*` files preserve real HTTP responses and request metadata; Zambeze JSON bodies use the helper’s `.html` filename convention. The public JavaScript evidence is bounded to its first 3 MB and marked truncated; the relevant route definitions are present. Failed probes retain metadata and any returned body; no origin body exists when a proxy tunnel fails. Some discovery sources were indexed/cached. The machine-readable policy and source fields preserve these distinctions.

Public access, lending rights, reuse permissions and live circulation remain separate questions. No accounts, persisted cookies, authentication, holds, challenge bypasses, TLS bypasses, outreach or app changes were used. The anonymous Koha pages contain ordinary login-form fields; they were neither submitted nor used as reusable credentials.
