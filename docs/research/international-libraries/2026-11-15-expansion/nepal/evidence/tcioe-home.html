Message from the Head, Library Section

Welcome to the Thapathali Campus Library. Our library exists for one purpose: to put the right book, journal or thesis in your hands at the moment you need it. Whether you are preparing for exams, working on a project or exploring beyond the syllabus, the collection and our staff are here for you.

I encourage every student and faculty member to make full use of the catalog, the digital resources and the reading spaces. Your suggestions help us grow, so do share them with us at the desk or through the portal.

Ms. Seema Thapa Giri Head, Library Section

About the library

Thapathali Engineering Campus was established in 2023 B.S. as a constituent campus of the Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University. The campus library has served since its establishment, growing alongside the campus as it expanded to bachelor programs in industrial, civil, electronics and communication, mechanical, architecture and automobile engineering, and master programs including earthquake engineering.

Today the collection supports teaching, learning and research across every engineering field taught at the campus, covering recent technological developments alongside the standard curriculum.
