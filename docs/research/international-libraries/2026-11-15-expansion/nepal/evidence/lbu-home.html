About the Library

The Central Library of Lumbini Buddhist University serves as the intellectual and academic heart of the university, dedicated to preserving and advancing the rich heritage of Buddhist philosophy, culture, and scholarship.

Our collection encompasses thousands of books, journals, theses, and rare manuscripts spanning Buddhist studies, philosophy, religion, Pali and Sanskrit literature, social sciences, humanities, law, and natural sciences.

We are committed to fostering a culture of research, critical thinking, and lifelong learning, nurturing students, researchers, and faculty in their journey of knowledge and enlightenment.

Basic Library Rules
  • Maintain silence inside the library premises.
  • Carry your valid University ID card at all times.
  • Return borrowed books on or before the due date.
  • Food and beverages are not allowed inside.
  • Handle books with care; do not mark or damage materials.
  • Mobile phones must be on silent mode while in the library.
