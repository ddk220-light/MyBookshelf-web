About Us

Welcome to the Alliance Française Library  — a place to explore, learn, and connect with all things French!

In our space, you’ll find:
  • 5,000+ books on everything from literature to culture
  • 285 DVDs with French films (many with English subtitles)
  • 50+ board games
  • A video game station with a PlayStation and 5 different games


Our library has something for everyone, whether you’re just starting out in French or a fluent speaker. You’ll find French literature, a huge comic book collection, magazines and newspapers, and plenty of DVDs to explore.

For students, we’ve got “La Bibliothèque de l’Apprenant”, a section specially curated to help with your studies. And don’t worry about devices— our computers are here for anyone to use. 

Drop by to explore, learn, or just enjoy some quiet time with a good book.
