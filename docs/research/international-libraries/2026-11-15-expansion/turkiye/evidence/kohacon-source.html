Why Istanbul?

Executive Summary: Technical Authority & Strategic Bridge

Turkey is not merely a venue provider, but a technical authority hosting one of the world's largest centralized Koha installations with over 1,300 branches and 30 million materials. Istanbul serves as a strategic bridge connecting continents and code, embodied by the Rami Library's "Living Library" concept.

No Vendor Lock-in

Open source ensures data freedom and prevents commercial monopolies.

Data Sovereignty

Aligns with the global movement for nations to own their cultural data.

Sustainability

Zero license fees for 1,300 libraries. A sustainable model.

Global Accessibility

Easy visa policies (e-Visa) and non-stop flights from 120+ countries make it accessible for the entire community.

Unmatched Value

Premium accommodation and world-class hospitality at significantly competitive costs for international participants.

Strategic Bridge

A unique chance to expand Koha's presence in the Middle East and Central Asia through Istanbul's leadership.

The Scale Challenge

1,300 Libraries, 1 Instance

Most large Koha setups are consortiums of independent databases. In Turkey, we run 1,300+ libraries on a single centralized Koha instance.

A standard 6-day KohaCon experience adapted for Istanbul.

Conference

Keynotes, lightning talks, and roundtables hosted at the Rami Library main seminar halls. Fully hybrid and streamed globally.
