Z39.50 Configuration

-A A +A

Records in KOHA originating in the library system may be searched using the z39.50 protocol.

From September 2025 METU Libraries are using a new library system and the z39.50 connection details have changed to:

Host: 144.122.201.156

Port: 2100

Database: biblios

Syntax: MARC21

 

The following attributes are supported:

  • Term
  • Author
  • Subject
  • Title
  • ISBN
  • ISSN
