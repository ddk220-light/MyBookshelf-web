Library Service

Borrowing and Circulation

Borrow, return and account for library materials according to published library rules.

Search Catalogue
Contact unitCirculation Desk
Service statusPublished

Overview

Circulation services support access to print and related library materials for eligible KIU users. Users should present a valid KIU identity document and observe loan periods, renewal conditions and recall notices.

Intended users

Registered KIU students, academic staff, administrative staff and other approved library users.

How to access this service

Confirm your borrower status, search the catalogue, locate the item, present it at the circulation desk and keep track of return dates through library guidance.

Requirements

Valid KIU identification and compliance with library borrowing rules.

Related support

Library staff can help you choose the correct service or route your request to the relevant unit.
