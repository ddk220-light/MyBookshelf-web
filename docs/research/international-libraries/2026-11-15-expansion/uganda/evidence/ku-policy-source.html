Membership

The following categories of membership shall be available.

INTERNAL- MEMBERSHIP

  1. Student Membership: Current students of Kampala University shall be entitled to student’s membership; a library card will be issued all the time when one wants to use the library services. The card has to be reviewed every year and one has to bring the old one with him or her for renewal. There will be a fine if one loses his or her card and it will also apply when one wants to clear at the end of the course ie 5,000 to be paid in the bank through accounts office
  2. Staff membership Current staff at the university shall be entitled to staff membership
  3. Reference membership Other persons may be granted reference membership at the discretion of a librarian. This membership shall be limited to consultation of materials within the library
  4. Membership Maintenance Abide by the rules and regulations and Renew membership periodically.
