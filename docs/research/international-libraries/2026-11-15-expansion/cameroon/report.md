# Cameroon: library expansion research

**15 November 2026: 4 observed book-query successes, 2 selected and 2 reserves; shortfall 8 selected institutions.** The selected networks are UCAC and UPAC. IIA and the GPE program library remain reserves because they are smaller or partial collections. Fourteen substantial institutions/networks remain visible in the priority list. A bounded recovery pass distinguished real catalog responses from an earlier execution-proxy outage; remaining gaps do not establish that infrastructure or books are absent.

**Recommendation:** PMB is the strongest evidenced cross-institution adapter opportunity, with four actual POST title searches and GET details across different templates. UCAC has concrete Nkolbisson copy evidence; UPAC has bibliographic records but an empty item table in the checked record. Preserve those differences. SIL remains a sourced Koha reuse candidate with unresolved access. No product or deployment changes were made.

## Market, networks and access

The opportunity spans Francophone and Anglophone state universities, private universities, national/legal-deposit and research libraries, municipal/associative reading services, and cultural-cooperation libraries. The [Ministry of Arts and Culture](https://minac.gov.cm/fr/?id=2&start=15&view=category) describes responsibility for national and decentralized libraries. Its [legal-deposit text](https://minac.gov.cm/fr/?id=2&start=25&view=category) names the National Library as a deposit institution. A [University of Montréal country chapter](https://rel.bib.umontreal.ca/bibliotheques-a-l-international1/chapter/cameroun/) provides scoped secondary context for these different sectors. None of these sources establishes a functioning national physical-book union catalog.

PMB has unusually useful named institutional evidence. A [2010 CERN–UNESCO paper by Yaoundé I’s central-library director](https://indico.cern.ch/event/114326/contributions/63127/attachments/46338/66652/tablerondebibliotheques.pdf) identifies implementation at Yaoundé I, Yaoundé II, Dschang and Buea, with CDS-ISIS/Winisis predecessors at Ngaoundéré and Yaoundé I. These are historical observations. [MINESUP’s 26 July 2023 report](https://www.minesup.gov.cm/index.php/2023/07/26/seminaire-de-formation-du-personne-de-la-sddar-a-lutilisation-du-logiciel-libre-de-gestion-de-la-bibliotheque-pmb-version-7-3/) documents PMB 7.3 training in its Documentation and Archives unit. Indexed institution-named PMB catalogs at UPAC, IIA, FATEB Yaoundé, FACTEC and GPE add deployment leads, not a national market-share estimate.

[SIL’s 2016 annual report](https://www.silcam.org/sites/cameroon/files/anrpt2016_e.pdf) establishes operational Koha and approximately 20,000 volumes at **Yaoundé**. Its [current service page](https://www.silcam.org/fr/developpement_des_langues/biblioth%C3%A8que-acad%C3%A9mique-sil-cameroun) identifies Yaoundé, Bamenda and Maroua and links `koha.silcam.org`. This is one institution/network; a selector alone would not verify three separate collections. [Ngaoundéré’s Faculty of Sciences](https://fs.univ-ndere.cm/realisation/) separately documents Biblio-FS, showing that a single national LMS assumption would be unsafe.

The [2007 EIFL newsletter](https://www.eifl.org/sites/default/files/resources/201505/28.pdf) documents COCUREL, the Consortium of Cameroon University and Research Libraries. Its current membership, shared LMS and physical-copy union endpoint were not verified. Electronic-resource cooperation, Research4Life access, institutional thesis repositories, Nossi’s indexed academic-deposit claims and Culturethèque should be kept separate from physical-book holdings. Claims of a 350,000-record national union on `gov.minac.site` / `minac-gouv.com` were not corroborated against established `minac.gov.cm`; they are excluded from accepted infrastructure and size claims.

Public discovery also does not grant borrowing rights. [Buea’s official rules](https://www.ubuea.cm/index.php/student-services/library/) allow external readers to consult on site and restrict borrowing to student-ID holders. [Yaoundé II’s 2017 library rules](https://site.univ-yaounde2.org/index.php/2017/10/12/la-bibliotheque-universitaire/) require authorization for other users and exclude home lending. UCAC allows registered outsiders to read on site, while loans and reservations are reserved for its staff and students. These institution-specific rules must remain visible; catalog visibility cannot be presented as universal borrowing access.

## Alternatives and unresolved scope

- **Goethe-Institut Cameroon, Yaoundé:** the [official lending page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/cm/en/bib/aus.html) confirms local printed media and links `yaounde.bibliothek.goethe.de`. Free on-site reading and paid borrowing membership are distinct. No current size was verified. Its reachable catalog origin publishes `Disallow: /`; search/detail probing stopped there.
- **ESIG:** an indexed Cameroon PMB catalog at `esig.cm/bibnum73/opac_css/` is another unquantified/possibly partial alternative, not a selected major institution.

- **IIA Yaoundé:** indexed PMB homepage reports 3,015 notices. It mixes printed books and electronic documents/student work; this catalog count is not a verified whole physical collection. The specific-title search and item detail succeeded after recovery; it remains a reserve pending substantial scale and coverage evidence.
- **FATEB Yaoundé / Byang Kato:** indexed PMB holdings name the Yaoundé library; section URLs mention 1,152 lines, not an audited collection total. Do not confuse this Cameroon campus with Bangui or count other campuses without filtered returned holdings. The recovery homepage returned HTTP500; stopped.
- **GPE program médiathèque:** actual Mankiw book and copy verified, but only within this Yaoundé II program. The mirrored 2025 activity report gives 4,995 mixed documents; it is not a university-wide physical-book count.
- **FACTEC and ISTM Maroua:** indexed PMB leads with unresolved scale/scope and initial tunnel errors. They were not rechecked in the bounded major-library recovery pass.
- **CLAC Mimboman/Yaoundé:** [BSF’s 2012 annual report](https://www.bibliosansfrontieres.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/bsf-rapport-annuel-2012.pdf) reports nearly 10,000 ouvrages and PMB support; [current BSF country work](https://www.bibliosansfrontieres.org/pays/cameroun/) includes this associative public library and mobile services. A current catalog endpoint was not established. Do not confuse this individual CLAC with a separately claimed OIF national network.
- **MINRESI, IMPM, IRGM and INC:** the historical study identifies sizeable specialized collections. Current public book-search endpoints remain unknown; the 2010 technology description must not be projected onto 2026.
- **CUIB digital library:** the indexed page showed 30 books. This small/digital observation is not evidence of a substantial whole physical-library catalog.

No authentication, TLS exception, challenge bypass, internal/private-IP probe, outreach, app edit or deployment was attempted. Failed checks mean unknown, never “not held.” Live circulation, public admission and metadata reuse rights remain separate verification tasks.
