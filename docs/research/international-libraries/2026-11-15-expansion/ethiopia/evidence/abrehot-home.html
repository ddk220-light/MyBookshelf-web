Chapter V — Executive Leadership

The people holding the next page open.

The library stands under the patronage of the nation and the city, and is held day to day by a board and an executive team. Move through the plates to read each remit.

Under their patronage

  • H.E Taye Atske Selassie

    President of Ethiopia

  • H.E Abiy Ahmed (Phd)

    Prime Minister of Ethiopia

    Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed initiated the Abrehot Library project, envisioned as a landmark center for knowledge, reading, and enlightenment. Under his initiative, the library was developed into a major public knowledge institution in Addis Ababa, officially inaugurated in January 2022.

  • H.E Adanech Abiebie

    Mayor of Addis Ababa

    As Mayor of Addis Ababa, H.E. Adanech Abiebie has supported the continued development of Abrehot Library as a center for reading, research, and public knowledge. She has taken part in the library’s institutional development and championed its ongoing modernization and digital transformation.
