General Collection

The Library collections include; books, periodicals, pamphlets, maps, microfiches, CD-ROMs, bibliographic databases, videotapes, and on-line full text databases. The Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC) provides an interface to search and browse the Library's physical information resources of about 90,000 titles and can be accessed at:  http://libcat.uneca.org/
