National Archives and Library of Ethiopia

The National Library of Ethiopia today holds more than 800 items in its manuscript collection, chiefly in Ge'ez, Amharic, and Arabic. The UNESCO and EMML projects together microfilmed 165 Christian and secular codices from this instiution's holdings.
