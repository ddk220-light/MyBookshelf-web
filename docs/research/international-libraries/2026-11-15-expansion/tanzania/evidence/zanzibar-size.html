How to Get Started

Access ZLSB's online services in four easy steps — no technical knowledge required.

1

Register Online

Visit register.php and fill in the simple form. Membership is completely free for all citizens of Zanzibar.

2

Get Your Card

After online approval, visit any ZLSB branch to collect your membership card. Children under 12 register with a parent or guardian.

3

Search & Browse

Use the OPAC to search for books, explore e-resources, and download documents — from any device, at any time of day.

4

Borrow & Renew

Reserve books online, then collect from your nearest branch. Use your member account to renew loans and manage your reading list.
