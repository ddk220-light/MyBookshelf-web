Welcome to KIRC - Your Gateway to Academic Excellence

Your gateway to comprehensive academic resources, research materials, and digital collections. The KIRC houses the university Library which is largely open-access with free shelf browsing and borrowing.

Explore Our Services Visit Us Today

About KIRC

The Knowledge and Information Resource Center (KIRC) houses the university Library which is largely open-access. Readers have free access to the shelves and may browse and/or borrow books at will.

Membership

DarTU Members: Free access after registration

Non-DarTU Users: Monthly membership available

Monthly Fee: TZS 10,000

Special Collections

Books and materials in special reserve or reference collections are not available for borrowing. These are highly valued, rare materials with wide use by both staff and students.

Library Collections

Our collection is divided into two main sections to serve different academic needs

Open Access Collection

Borrowing Limits:

Students: Up to 3 books

Academic Staff: Up to 5 books

Loan Period: 2 weeks

Special Reserve Collection

High-Demand Materials:

3-hour loan period

In-library use only

Note: These materials are not available for home borrowing due to high demand and limited availability.
