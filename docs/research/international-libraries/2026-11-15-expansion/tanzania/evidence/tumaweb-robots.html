Vice Chancellor Welcome Note

Dear Students, Parents, Guardians, Partners, and Friends,

Tumaini University Makumira is accredited by TCU as an ELCT-owned university established as an educational institution since 1947. Our motto is ‘University for education, research, teaching and service.’ This slogan reflects the university’s commitment to providing quality education, demonstrated through excellence in research, teaching, and service to society. According to its mission and vision, the university aims for creative and innovative activities, driven by an aspiration for impactful education, love, stewardship, professionalism, accountability, integrity, and dignity. I invite you to discover TUMA. Get to know the people who make TUMA exceptional, explore options for studying and education, and learn more about research priorities and collaborations. We encourage your curiosity to find a place at TUMA and become part of the innovative initiatives we are advancing. We welcome your inspiration, visiting us and becoming part of TUMA.

We cordially welcome you!
