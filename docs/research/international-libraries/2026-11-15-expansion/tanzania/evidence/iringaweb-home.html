WELCOME TO THE UNIVERSITY OF IRINGA LIBRARY

A hub designed to support teaching, learning, research, and community engagement across the University. The Library is committed to providing reliable, accessible, and user-friendly information materials and services that enrich academic excellence.

Our collection includes a wide range of electronic and print information materials covering both local and international research. To ensure effective access and use of these resources, the Library continues to invest in skilled staff, modern technologies, and up-to-date facilities. Our dedicated team is always ready to assist users in finding, retrieving, and using information effectively.

In addition, the Library offers a comfortable and well-equipped learning environment with adequate reading space, computer facilities, and quiet study areas to support individual study, research, and academic growth.
