# Tanzania: library expansion feasibility, 15 November 2026

**Five observed institutions/networks; five selected, zero reserves; shortfall of five against the ten-target goal.** Four successes use Koha; Zanzibar Library Services Board uses a custom OPAC. Nineteen substantial priorities were assessed, with additional specialized routes considered. Search-index records and catalog homepages were never counted as successful queries.

A policy-aware Koha integration is the clearest near-term route through SNAL, SUZA, MUST and DarTU. TLSB remains the largest public-network opportunity in this screen, but its public website links to an iNDLS login route and this research did not establish an anonymous physical-book search. The [15 October 2026 TLSB update](https://www.tlsb.go.tz/news/tlsb-board-chairperson-commends-management-on-indls-development-directs-expansion-of-services-to-all-43-libraries-nationwide) states that iNDLS is used in **11 libraries**, with expansion to all **43** directed. This is not proof of 43 searchable collections.

## Marketplace, software and access

Mainland public/national provision is organized through TLSB; Zanzibar has its own public library board. University libraries include large public and private institutions and specialized teaching/research networks. Their membership rules differ: DarTU advertises external membership at TZS 10,000/month; ZLSB advertises free standard membership for Zanzibaris, three books for fourteen days. Anonymous OPAC access does not establish a visitor’s borrowing eligibility. See the saved [DarTU service page](evidence/dartu-size.html) and [ZLSB service page](evidence/zanzibar-size.html).

[COTUL, described by EIFL](https://www.eifl.info/country/tanzania), is a high-leverage coordination, licensing and training network covering academic, research and special libraries. EIFL lists 96 members and separately says 47 institutions in its work; those are distinct scopes. No shared national physical-book union catalog was verified. Licensed electronic journals, ebook databases and institutional repositories must remain separate from local copy holdings.

Koha is well evidenced by the four working deployments, Ardhi’s official library description, and a [2019 UDSM dissertation](https://libraryrepository.udsm.ac.tz/bitstreams/07f1b039-2656-4b06-9530-359ce03d9539/download) that purposively examined OUT, IFM and CBE as Koha adopters. This supports adoption, not a national market-share percentage. Historical ABCD reports for Sokoine/Mzumbe do not override current Koha pages. Makumira identifies Mandarin Oasis and its endpoint identifies Mandarin 5.7.11; its live page displayed “Startup failed.” ZLSB’s custom template does not establish a named backend LMS.

## Request evidence and integration implications

### Sokoine National Agricultural Library (Sokoine University of Agriculture)

`GET http://www.snal.sua.ac.tz/cgi-bin/koha/opac-search.pl?idx=ti&q=Research+methodology+in+social+sciences&count=5`

Checked `2026-11-15T07:46:56.703402+00:00`; HTTP 200; final URL `http://www.snal.sua.ac.tz/cgi-bin/koha/opac-search.pl?idx=ti&q=Research+methodology+in+social+sciences&count=5`. Server reports four results: two related-title Methodology of research in social sciences rows and two exact-title rows. Selected record 119099 was opened separately.

Evidence: [query response](evidence/sua-query2.html), [request metadata](evidence/sua-query2.json), [robots metadata](evidence/sua-robots.json). Anonymous catalog search works. Registration and SNAL borrowing rules apply; public search does not establish external borrowing eligibility.

Existing Koha adapter reuse is a candidate, not tested compatibility. Handle single-result redirects, retain edition/biblionumber identity, preserve reference/special-reserve restrictions, and check template-specific holding labels.

Official catalog is linked and served over HTTP; no HTTPS upgrade or TLS bypass was attempted. Search returned duplicate-looking records; retain biblionumbers and do not merge editions from titles.

## Limits and reproducibility

- Five selected successful institutions/networks; explicit five-target shortfall after investigating major libraries and meaningful alternatives.
- No national largest-ten ranking established. Differing dates, book/copy/title/material units and institutional proxies are not comparable rankings.
- Koha has strong scoped evidence; no national LMS shares estimated.
- TLSB iNDLS currently 11 libraries in dated official evidence, not full43; no open physical query verified.
- ZLSB 50,000+ title marketing claim conflicts with OPAC2,002+; In Collection is not item-level availability.
- MUST selection uses a university-wide institutional proxy with no numeric size. DarTU size is indexed official evidence, not a directly fetched PDF.
- Only small public GET searches and detail reads were performed; no app adapter, live circulation, membership, holds, credentials or authenticated flows tested.
- Failed requests or robots exclusions do not mean an institution lacks a book.
- Tuma catalog session URLs and ephemeral form tokens are redacted from stored bodies/metadata; retain fresh public-form recipe rather than token reuse.

The shared `../probe.py` performed bounded GET requests under the transparent MyBookshelfResearch user agent, normal TLS validation, small independent-origin batches. Each catalog was checked against robots first. A404 means no published rules were observed, not affirmative reuse permission. No contact, authentication, account creation, hold, reservation or product change occurred.

Saved responses are bounded; anonymous form tokens and the failed Mandarin session identifier were redacted after collection. Metadata retains the original response hash and a separate sanitized artifact hash where redaction occurred. Cookies were never persisted. Reproduction starts from the official public form rather than reusing a captured session or nonce. Source discovery exports are retained as contextual evidence; they are not successful catalog responses.
