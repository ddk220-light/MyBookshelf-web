    National Library of Sri Lanka

    A National Resource for Research, Reference and Knowledge

    The National Library of Sri Lanka is the country’s principal national library and a central institution for the collection, preservation and provision of access to Sri Lanka’s documentary and intellectual heritage. Functioning primarily as a research and reference library, it serves scholars, researchers, academics, students, professionals and all others seeking reliable information resources to support inquiry, scholarship and lifelong learning.

    As a library of last resort, the National Library has a distinctive role within the nation’s information infrastructure: to provide access to resources and information that may not be readily available through other libraries and information services. Its mission extends beyond the provision of resources within its own collections to supporting access to knowledge throughout Sri Lanka through library networks, information centres and resource centres.

    The Library’s collections encompass Sri Lankan publications, reference works, periodicals, newspapers, government publications, theses and dissertations, rare and special collections, maps, audiovisual materials and other resources in a range of formats.

    Through legal deposit, acquisition, exchange, gifts and donations, the Library develops and preserves a documentary record of the country’s published output, supporting the study of Sri Lanka’s history, society, culture, language and intellectual life.

    Beyond its collections, the National Library provides reference and information services that assist users in identifying, locating and making effective use of relevant resources. Its services support bibliographic and subject inquiries, research, current awareness, specialised information needs and access to information for readers with visual impairments. Through these activities, the Library contributes to the advancement of scholarship, the dissemination of knowledge and the preservation of the nation’s intellectual heritage for present and future generations.

    "The National Library of Sri Lanka welcomes all who seek to explore its collections and use its resources for research, reference, education and informed inquiry."

    National Library Services

    Reading and Reference Services

    Access books, journals, newspapers, government publications, reference works and specialised collections for study and research.

    Research and Information Inquiries

    Obtain assistance with bibliographic, factual, subject-based and research-related inquiries.

    Interlibrary and Networked Resource Access

    Explore opportunities to identify and access information resources through participating libraries and information networks, subject to applicable arrangements.

    Current Awareness Services

    Keep informed of new acquisitions and developments in relevant information resources.

    Selective Dissemination of Information (SDI)

    Receive information selected according to established research interests and individual information needs.

    Reprographic Services

    Access photocopying, scanning, printing and electronic-copy services, subject to copyright provisions and applicable conditions.

    User Education and Orientation

    Develop familiarity with the Library’s catalogue, collections, facilities and information services through user education programmes and guided visits.

    Accessible Information Services

    Access specialised information resources and services intended to support readers with visual impairments.

    Information Repackaging Services

    Consult curated collections of information resources assembled to support assignments, projects and research on selected subjects.

    Explore the Catalogue

    Search the National Library’s holdings to identify books and other catalogued resources relevant to your subject, research question or information need.

    Note to Researchers & Readers

    As the nation’s archival repository and library of last resort, the National Library of Sri Lanka is a closed-access reference library. Materials identified through this OPAC are for in-library consultation only and may be requested at the respective Reading Room service desks using the item's Call Number. For remote access to digitized heritage materials, please visit the National Digital Library Repository .
