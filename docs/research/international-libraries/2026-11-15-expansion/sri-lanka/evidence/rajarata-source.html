Online Public Access Catalogue

Welcome to the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka’s Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC). Our OPAC is a user-friendly online platform designed to provide easy and convenient access to the university library’s vast collection of resources.

At Rajarata University, we believe that access to information is vital for academic excellence and research advancement. Our OPAC allows students, faculty members, and researchers to explore a wide range of materials, including books, journals, theses, and multimedia resources.

We encourage all members of the Rajarata University community to make the most of our Online Public Access Catalogue. Visit our OPAC today to explore our extensive collection, enrich your research, and enhance your academic journey. Should you require any assistance, our dedicated library staff is always ready to provide guidance and support.
