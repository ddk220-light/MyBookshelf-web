NATIONAL LIBRARY OF SRI LANKA

The National Library of Sri Lanka is the country's foremost research and reference library, dedicated to preserving the nation's documentary heritage and providing access to knowledge resources. Through this catalogue, users can discover books, journals, government publications, rare materials, and other information resources held within the National Library's collections.
