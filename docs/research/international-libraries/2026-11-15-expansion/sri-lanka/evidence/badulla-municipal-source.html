Leadership

Welcome Messages

Chairman

T.M Kumarathissa

"As Mayor of Badulla, I am honored to share our commitment to building a city that reflects both the aspirations of our people and the progressive vision of Sri Lanka’s new government policy. At the heart of this journey is our dedication to responsible waste management—creating a cleaner, greener Badulla through recycling, reducing pollution, and protecting the environment for generations to come. Equally, we are stepping confidently into the digital future. By expanding e-governance and online services, we are making municipal services more transparent, efficient, and accessible, ensuring that every citizen benefits from technology and innovation. Together, let us move forward with unity and determination to create a sustainable, smart, and vibrant city—a Badulla that we can all be proud to call home."

Secretary

D.M.K.D Dissanayake

"It is a privilege to serve the people of Badulla at a time when our city is embracing both tradition and transformation. Inspired by Sri Lanka’s new government policy, we are committed to building a community that is sustainable, people-focused, and ready for the future. Our waste management initiatives are not just about cleaner streets—they are about creating a healthier environment, protecting our natural resources, and instilling a sense of responsibility in all of us. Alongside this, we are moving confidently into the digital era, where technology and innovation will make our services more efficient, transparent, and accessible to every citizen. Together, with the dedication of our staff and the support of our people, we can shape a Badulla that is greener, smarter, and stronger—a city that inspires pride today and hope for tomorrow."
