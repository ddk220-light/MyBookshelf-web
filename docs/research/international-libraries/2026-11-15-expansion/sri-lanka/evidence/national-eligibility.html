Who Can Use The Library

Students and Academics

Access study materials, academic journals, and resources to support learning, teaching, and research.

Researchers and Professionals

Utilize specialized collections, data, and references for in-depth research and professional development.

Government Officers and Policy Makers

Find reliable information, reports, and archives to support decision-making and policy development.

School Children and Lifelong Learners

Explore books, educational programs, and resources that encourage reading and continuous learning.

General Public and Foreign Visitors

Enjoy access to a wide range of books, cultural materials, and information about local heritage and knowledge.

Ignites Minds and Inspire Future

The National Library of Sri Lanka functions primarily as a research and reference library and serves as the country’s library of last resort.
