OUSL Library Network

The Open University of Sri Lanka Library network operates across the country, comprising the Main Central Library at Nawala and 08 Regional Centre Libraries. We provide open, flexible, and lifelong learning support with a comprehensive repository of printed books, peer-reviewed e-journals, multimedia resources, and specialized research assistance.

Leadership Message

Welcome Message From Librarian

Welcome to the Library of The Open University of Sri Lanka.

As an integral part of the University's Open and Distance Learning system, the Library is committed to supporting flexible, accessible, and lifelong learning opportunities for students across the country and beyond. We recognize the diverse needs of our learners and strive to provide equitable access to quality information resources, digital collections, learning support services, and innovative technologies that facilitate independent study and academic success.

The library plays a vital role in advancing the University's mission of teaching, learning, research, and community engagement. Through a user-centered approach, we continuously enhance our services and resources to meet the evolving needs of students, academics, researchers, and staff in an increasingly digital learning environment.

As Librarian, I am committed to fostering a dynamic, inclusive, and responsive library that empowers learners, supports scholarly excellence, and promotes knowledge creation. Through collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement, we aim to strengthen the library's contribution to the University's vision as a leader in Open and Distance Learning.

I invite you to explore our wide range of resources and services and to engage with our dedicated library team as we work together to support your learning, teaching, and research journey.
