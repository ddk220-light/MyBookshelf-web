    About Library

    General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU) is a unique university. Its teaching and learning behavior, system of student nurturing, high level of discipline, and grooming the students for a multi task, productivity-based, high flown job market support the ‘graduate profile’ of the university. The KDU library positioned itself as the driving force to elevate this graduate profile by facilitating teaching, learning and research process of the university and advancement of knowledge of the KDU community as a whole.
