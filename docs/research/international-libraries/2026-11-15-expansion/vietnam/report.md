# Vietnam — library expansion evidence, 15 November 2026

**11 institutions returned identifiable books through actual public searches: 10 selected targets and one reserve.** The selection includes the national library, three public-library institutions and six university collections. This is a feasibility shortlist, not Vietnam’s ten largest libraries. Two university selections use explicit institutional proxies rather than an unverified book-stock total. Large blocked institutions remain priorities below.

The most promising immediate work is a **UCVN adapter for the National Library, plus reusable KIPOS and Lạc Việt Vebrary adapters**. KIPOS searches succeeded independently at Hải Phòng University and Nha Trang University; Vebrary succeeded at the Hue central learning-resource collection and HUSC. PSC zLIS exposes an anonymous JSON search at Bình Dương University. Existing Koha code is a reuse candidate, but the verified HUAF installation is retained as a reserve because its copy rows explicitly say **“OPAC thử nghiệm”**. None of these were tested inside MyBookshelf.

[findings.json](findings.json) contains the common schema, exact query text, semantic parameters, UTC timestamps, IDs, source references and access notes. Each successful request has a bounded raw response and an exact request metadata file under [evidence/](evidence/). [source-notes.json](source-notes.json) records scoped, paraphrased supporting-source observations. Failed requests are retained and excluded from the success count.

## Marketplace and network leverage

Vietnam’s public-library and university sectors need several adapter families. [Tinh Vân’s Libol page](https://tinhvan.vn/en/products/software-packages/libol/) lists major public and university customers, and [CMC describes iLib8](https://cmcts.com.vn/en/solutions/giai-phap-thu-vien-dien-tu-thu-vien-so.html) for public, university and specialist libraries. These are evidence of local commercial product presence, not a current market-share census. Their historical client lists must not override live platform evidence: Hanoi’s [current institutional introduction names SLIB](https://ttvanhoavathuvien.hanoi.gov.vn/gioi-thieu-chi-tiet/gioi-thieu-ve-trung-tam-van-hoa-va-thu-vien-ha-noi-1.html), Bình Dương now documents PSC zLIS7, and the National Library’s current catalog identifies UCVN.

[D&L’s 2020/2021 Koha description](https://dlcorp.com.vn/phan-mem-quan-ly-tai-nguyen-truyen-thong-ma-nguon-mo-koha/) claims more than 30 customers across **Vietnam and Southeast Asia together**. It does not establish 30 Vietnamese installations. The observed sample also contains Vebrary, KIPOS, SLIB, PSC zLIS and custom interfaces. No defensible nationally dominant LMS or national share percentage was established.

Public-library catalogs provide broad discovery, while university borrowing and licensed content generally depend on institutional accounts and reader rules. No reader eligibility was inferred from search access. HCMC’s guide distinguishes reading-room use, home lending, mobile-library-only stock and technical processing; those meanings must survive normalization. At Hanoi, “Cho phép” appears under the **reservation-permission column**, not an availability column. At HUFLIT, an available-looking count is explicitly a nightly snapshot.

## Reproduction and interpretation

Requests were bounded public research requests, with no accounts, holds, outreach, bot bypass or disabled TLS. At most three different origins were fetched concurrently. Ordinary in-memory anonymous cookie jars were used for normal form sessions and were not persisted. Transient ASP.NET state remains in exact request metadata where needed, but is not a durable replay credential. Reproduction requires fetching a fresh form. Failed no-session/malformed requests are retained alongside the successful flow.

The reusable recipes are:

- **UCVN:** fresh GET `/tim-kiem`; extract the public anti-forgery field and send its value in `RequestVerificationToken`; POST `/Search` with `type=quick`, title option `ti`, exact keyword and pageSize 5. The successful semantic payload and full response are retained. Opaque record IDs must not be interpreted as publication or capture dates.
- **KIPOS:** fresh GET the official `keywordsearch.aspx?mnuid=142`; preserve ASP.NET hidden fields; submit `txtsearchValue1`, title field `t`, and `btnSearch`. NTU additionally sets `ddlMaterialType=1`, its observed physical-copy filter. Original generated control names and full submitted state are in the metadata.
- **Vebrary:** fresh anonymous GET the observed search form, POST `Search_Count` with `lstValue` containing the query and four apostrophe separators, then POST `SearchPaging` with returned source ID and pageSize 5 in the **same session**. Query chains are explicit in JSON. The catalog itself exposes verbose backend query text; none was constructed or submitted by this research. Initial detail HTML zeros are unloaded components, not evidence of no holdings.
- **Hanoi/site 2 Khanh Hoa:** submit ordinary ASP.NET title forms with fresh hidden state; Khanh Hoa also requires the anonymous session for the result redirect. Keep legacy holding-library names and present institutional names separately.
- **PSC zLIS:** use the observed same-origin public `/api/Book/SearchOpac` route; title phrase/right-truncation descriptor plus date sort `dsc`. Copy count and file count have separate meanings.
- **HUFLIT:** wait for the completed streamed HTML; the first loading block is not the whole response. Retain the source’s snapshot timestamp.

Catalog robots 404 means only that no published robots file was found. National Library robots 200 is a manually inspected ordinary not-found page, so its policy remains **unknown** under the protocol’s bounded-public-query exception. Khanh Hoa’s training-only crawler rules were not converted into permission for training or unrestricted reuse. The public Haiphong catalog’s literal public IPv4 URL came directly from the official homepage; its transport is HTTP, an integration limitation.

The two selected Hue collections are not a union-plus-member count: **LRC-HUE at20 Lê Lợi** and **LIB.HUSC at77 Nguyễn Huệ** have separate catalog source labels, record namespaces and returned holdings. The former serves the federation centrally, while the latter belongs to a member university. The research does not claim their bibliographic titles never overlap; it asserts distinct institution-owned copy collections. HUAF is a reserve. Likewise, public-library mergers do not make separate legacy endpoints independent institutions.

No national total was calculated from incomparable inventory dates, record counters, volumes, digital objects and institutional proxies. Public visibility establishes discoverability only; production reliability, current borrowing eligibility and reuse terms remain integration work. One mistyped `lic.hueuni.edu.vn` detail hostname failed before correction to the observed `lrc.hueuni.edu.vn`; the failed response is retained and unused. All selected counts rest on actual successful search responses, not that correction or a standalone indexed record.
