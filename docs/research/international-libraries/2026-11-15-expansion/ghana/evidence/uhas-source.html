Catalogue

  1. Home
  2. Catalogue

The UHAS Library Catalogue is an online tool that allows our library users to search and locate books, journals, and other materials in the library’s collection. It provides real-time availability and access information for efficient resource discovery. With our catalogue, our library users can access the over fourteen thousand (14,000) collection of print materials now. Through our library catalogue, you can search for these valuable books by typing in a keyword, author, or title.
