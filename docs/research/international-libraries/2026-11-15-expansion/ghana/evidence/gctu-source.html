Overview

About the Library

On behalf of Ghana Communication Technology University (GCTU) Libraries, we extend a warm welcome to students, faculty members and staff.

The Library’s website is our gateway to our resources and services. It provides a brief introduction to the facilities and services provided by the library, library operating hours and other basic information pertaining to the library.

The primary objective of the library is to implement, enrich, and support the educational programmes of GCTU.
