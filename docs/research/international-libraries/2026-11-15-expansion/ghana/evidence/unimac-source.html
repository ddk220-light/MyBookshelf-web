Slide 1

Welcome to the University of Media, Arts and Communication (UniMAC) Library — your gateway to knowledge, creativity, and research excellence. Our libraries are dedicated to supporting teaching, learning, and innovation by providing access to diverse print and electronic resources, cutting-edge technologies, and expert guidance. We invite you to explore, discover, and connect with information that empowers your academic and creative journey at UniMAC.
Mrs. Lydia Nyantakyi-Baah (University Librarian)

UniMAC Library OPAC

UniMAC Institutional Repository

A digital archive dedicated to collecting, preserving, and showcasing the scholarly works, research outputs, and creative projects of the University of Media, Arts and Communication.

Slide 3
Research Commons

The Research & Knowledge Commons at the University of Media, Arts and Communication (UniMAC) is a dedicated research support space within the UniMAC Library designed to assist students, faculty, and researchers in their scholarly work.
