# Ghana — catalog feasibility and expansion priorities

Research checked **15 November 2026 UTC**. **Four catalogs returned real books or a book-length government report: two selected institutional targets and two reserves.** Three expose physical item rows; UPSA exposes book metadata with **No copy data**. The target of ten substantial selected institutions was **not met: eight remain unverified**. None was integrated into MyBookshelf.

The selected targets are **USTED / AAMUSTED Library** and **Parliament of Ghana Library**. Selection uses explicit institutional-scale proxies: a two-campus public university and a national legislative research library. Their observed catalogs do not establish that they rank among Ghana's ten largest collections. WIUC remains a useful Koha reserve with unquantified current stock; UPSA is a digital-catalog reserve with no verified physical holdings. The [machine-readable dossier](findings.json) preserves all exact requests, timestamps, holdings, source qualifications and policies.

## Market and integration recommendation

Ghana has a centralized public-library network under Ghana Library Authority (GhLA), separately operated university libraries and specialist research collections. IFLA's country profile says Ghana has no national library and that special libraries perform some national-library functions. It describes CARLIGH as supporting training and shared electronic resources while individual academic libraries remain under their parent institutions. Public, university and special-library access rules therefore cannot be assumed to be identical. [IFLA country profile](https://librarymap.ifla.org/countries/ghana?print_country=1) ([saved evidence](evidence/ifla-source.html)).

**Koha is the best supported first adapter family in this screen.** All three catalogs with physical item rows here use Koha. Official pages also identify it at UENR, UniMAC and CSIR-FORIG; UDS's prohibited search path is a Koha route. This is a feasibility-selected sample, not a national LMS market-share estimate. A librarian-authored 2017 study documents Regent's 2015 Koha implementation and CARLIGH's Koha workshops; its historical observations cannot determine current national adoption. [Regent study](https://lirgjournal.org.uk/index.php/lir/article/download/764/758/3381) ([PDF](evidence/regent-study.pdf), [text](evidence/regent-study.txt)); [UENR](https://uenrlbr.uenr.edu.gh/); [UniMAC cataloging unit](https://library.unimac.edu.gh/cataloguing-classification-unit); [CSIR-FORIG](https://csir-forig.org.gh/library/).

The existing MyBookshelf Koha adapter is a reuse candidate, with template and branch mapping still to validate. USTED's results call both copies available for loan while its detail distinguishes **Reserve Book Shelf** and **Lending Book Shelf**. Parliament similarly displays **Available** alongside **Reference**. These meanings must survive parsing. Public lookup does not confer take-home rights, realtime circulation assurance or a metadata reuse licence.

UPSA runs **SLiMS 9 (Bulian)** and needs a separate adapter if pursued. Its actual record has no copy data and an ebook attachment. The form requires the submit-button parameter `search=search`; omitting it returns the home form. Even a later exact phrase query returned zero for a title present in the subject results, so search semantics need care. No ebook attachment was downloaded. UG's advertised UGCat/Sierra-Millennium family and historical UEW Virtua require revalidation once allowed and reachable; [Pentecost's official guide](https://pentvars.edu.gh/research/welcome-to-our-library/library-help/) names Destiny, another distinct family.

### Shared-service opportunities

- **GhLA offers the largest public-network opportunity.** Its acting director reported 139 public libraries in September 2025. The official website's current public catalog component uses an OPDS digital feed. One allowed, anonymous `Things fall apart` request timed out. No physical branch holding was proved, and the network cannot be counted as 139 verified institutions. [Reported network scale](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-has-only-139-public-libraries-authority-cries-for-more.html), [official frontend](https://www.library.gov.gh/), [request evidence](evidence/ghla-opds-query.json).
- **CARLIGH is a coordination opportunity**, particularly for access terms and common Koha templates. Its official services emphasize electronic resources, training and cooperation. No nationwide physical-book union was established. Consortium membership is not a verified holding and cannot be counted as an integration. [CARLIGH](https://www.carligh.org/) ([saved](evidence/carligh-source.html)).
- **`ghlibrary.online` is shared hosting, not a verified union.** UHAS and Presbyterian official pages link distinct port-based tenants. Both tenants' robots files prohibit automated access. Catholic University's indexed homepage also points to the host, but its precise tenant route was not established. Shared hosting may simplify an eventual agreement or template; it does not override institutional attribution or policy. [UHAS](https://library.uhas.edu.gh/catalogue), [Presbyterian](https://www.presbyuniversity.edu.gh/site/e-services/).

## Policies, evidence and limits

Robots was fetched before every catalog query/detail origin; the shared longest-match parser was called before each query and detail. The [policy audit](evidence/policy-audit.json) records policy decisions for successful and blocked paths. UDS's longer search exclusion wins over `Allow: /`. Ashesi's explicit ChatGPT-User and OAI-SearchBot exclusions were respected. UEW, UHAS and Presbyterian prohibit `/`. A timeout, HTTP503 or transport403 did not clear a catalog for querying.

USTED's robots allows the applicable research paths and also supplies content signals `search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference`; these do not establish a general reuse licence. WIUC, Parliament and UPSA returned HTTP404 for robots; this is absence of published robots rules, not affirmative permission. GhLA's website robots returned the same ordinary Angular home HTML as its root, with no challenge. This documented SPA fallback allowed limited reads of already-linked public JavaScript assets. The independent OPDS origin had interpretable permissive robots; its one title query timed out. A public configuration contained an API key; it was **not used** and is redacted from retained evidence.

Bounded raw responses and request metadata are retained in `evidence/`. The PDF study is retained as a PDF with an offline text extraction. No cookies, accounts or browser state were persisted; no authentication, holds, restricted-route queries, private-address requests, challenge/TLS bypass, outreach, full-text downloads, app edits or deployment occurred. Public anonymous HTML contains transient form fields but they were not reused as credentials.

This is a compatibility and opportunity screen, not a completed integration or proof of nationwide availability. **The observed result is two selected targets and two reserves, leaving a shortfall of eight against the selected-target goal.**
