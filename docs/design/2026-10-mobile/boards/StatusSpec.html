Unknown is never “no”. Errors, timeouts and partial searches use the triangle or the dashed ring with words that say why. They never use the “all out” or “not listed” wording.

“Similar” never looks “exact”. Possible matches, shared ISBNs and title matches use the half-filled amber shape and always ask the reader to check the edition.

Slow only matters when it fails. No “checked” times or “may be out of date” lines. A result is shown as the latest; it refreshes each time the book is opened. If the catalog doesn't answer in time, say so plainly and offer Try again.
