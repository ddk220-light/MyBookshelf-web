Public library in Kotturpuram, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Search its catalog for a book and see whether a copy is on the shelf.

What MyBookshelf can check here

  • Whether a copy can be borrowed
  • How many copies, and where they are shelved
  • Call numbers
  • Search by title or author, or by ISBN

Results come live from the library's catalog when you open a book. We don't store them here.

How it works

Search for a book, open it, and we check this library's catalog for that edition and others. Save books to your shelf to check again later. No account needed; your shelf stays in your browser.
