How it works

1. Find a book

Search by title, author or ISBN, or scan the barcode with your phone. Add it to your reading shelf.

2. Choose your library

Pick the library you borrow from. We read its public catalog, the same one on its website.

3. See if it’s there

Each book says “In your library” or “Not in your library”. When we can’t check, we say so.

Built to be trusted

  • No account required and no ads. Your reading shelf stays in your browser, or in a free account if you make one.
  • Location is used only when you choose “Libraries near me”.
  • Open source: the code and the library list are public.
  • Independent. Libraries set their own borrowing rules.

Corrections and questions

Readers and libraries can ask for details to be corrected or removed. Write to us or open an issue with the library’s name and a source for the correction. To ask for a library to be added, use “My library isn’t listed” in the app.
