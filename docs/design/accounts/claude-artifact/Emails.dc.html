Account emails

Every email the account flows send, including edge cases. Emails use one light style in every reading light. Sender domain and support address are placeholders.

E1 · UX-01, UX-02Subject: Verify your email for MyBookshelfTo: reader@example.testFrom: MyBookshelf <accounts@[SENDING DOMAIN]>

Verify your email

Hi Reader Example, confirm this address to finish creating your MyBookshelf account.

Verify email

The link works for 24 hours and opens a page where you confirm. If you asked for more than one email, use the newest.

Didn't create an account? Ignore this email. Nothing happens unless you confirm.

Sent because this address was used to create a MyBookshelf account. MyBookshelf · [SUPPORT ADDRESS]

E2 · AC-02 duplicate sign-upSubject: Someone tried to create an account with your emailTo: reader@example.test

You already have an account

Someone tried to create a MyBookshelf account with this address. There's already an account for it.

Sign in

Forgot your password? Reset it. If this wasn't you, you don't need to do anything.

The sign-up page shows the same "Check your email" message either way, so it never reveals who has an account.

E3 · UX-04Subject: Reset your MyBookshelf passwordTo: reader@example.test

Reset your password

Use this link to choose a new password.

Choose a new password

It works once, for 30 minutes. Changing your password signs you out on every device.

Didn't ask for this? Ignore this email. Your password stays the same.

MyBookshelf · [SUPPORT ADDRESS]

E4 · AC-04 Google or Apple onlySubject: Signing in to MyBookshelfTo: reader@example.test

You sign in with Google

Someone asked to reset the password for this address. Your account doesn't have a password. You sign in with Google.

Sign in with Google

Want a password too? Sign in with Google, then go to Settings › Sign-in methods › Set password.

The reset page shows the same message for every address.

E5 · AC-04 unknown address No email is sent When nobody has an account with the address, nothing is sent. The page still says "If this address can receive a reset link, we'll send one," so it never reveals who has an account.

If this wasn't you, reset your password now.

E10 · Proposed, not in the PRDSubject: Google was connected to your MyBookshelf accountTo: reader@example.test

Google (reader@example.test) was connected to your MyBookshelf account on 1 November 2026.

If this wasn't you, sign in, disconnect it in Settings › Sign-in methods, and change your password.

The same email covers disconnecting a method and setting a password for the first time.

E11 · UX-10Subject: Your MyBookshelf account was deletedTo: reader@example.test

Your account was deleted

Your MyBookshelf account was deleted on 1 November 2026. You were signed out everywhere.

Your account data is removed within 24 hours, and from backups within 30 days. Books saved only in a browser stay in that browser until you clear them.

Didn't do this? Contact [SUPPORT ADDRESS]. A deleted account can't be restored.

This is the last email about this account.

E12 · Expired, used or replaced links No new email by itself Opening an expired, used or replaced link shows a page with "Send a new link" or "Request a new link". A new email goes only when the reader asks, within the resend limit.
