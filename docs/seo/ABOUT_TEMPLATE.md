### What we promise

- **Independent.** MyBookshelf isn't run by, paid by or affiliated with any
  library or bookshop.
- **Honest results.** We show what a library's catalog says. When we can't
  tell, we say so, and we never guess that a book is missing.
- **Private by default.** No account and no ads. Your shelf stays in your
  browser. Searches aren't stored. We use your location only when you tap Near
  me.
- **Open.** The code is public under Apache-2.0, and the library list under CC
  BY 4.0, on [GitHub link].
- **Respectful of libraries.** We identify ourselves to every catalog, keep
  requests light, and correct or remove details on request.

### How the library list is made

Each library comes from its own public website or catalog, added by hand or by
a published script. We record where each detail came from and when the catalog
connection was last checked. Place names come from GeoNames. [Link: how the
directory works on GitHub]
