MyBookshelf

A little help for your bookshelf

Find and save a book

Open Scan to scan a barcode or search by title, author, or ISBN. Open a book and choose Save to shelf.

Check your library

Choose a library from a book or in Settings. If availability isn’t shown, use View catalog to check the library’s website.

Camera not opening?

Allow camera access in your browser’s website settings, then try again. You can always search by title, author, or ISBN.

Your saved books

Guest books stay in this browser; clearing browser data removes them. An optional private website account can sync your selected library and saved books across browsers. Signing in does not automatically import a guest copy. Website accounts do not sync your native phone apps.

To empty the current shelf, use Settings → Clear saved books. When signed in this clears account saves; as a guest it clears this browser’s saved books. Review the confirmation before continuing.

Account and deletion help

Account settings contains private details, sign-in methods, signed-in devices, recently viewed controls, data download and account deletion. Account history is off until you turn it on. Verification and recovery links need an explicit continuation; opening an email link alone does not confirm it.

If a deletion response is uncertain, keep its status tab open and choose Check deletion status. Do not start another deletion just to check progress. A received request continues if the tab closes. Contact support if you lose the receipt or need help with delayed cleanup.

Need a hand? Email deepakdhanavel@gmail.com with your device, browser or app version, and what happened. Please don’t send library card numbers, PINs, passwords, verification/reset links, session cookies or deletion receipt nonces.
Help with the iPhone and Android apps

Keep your reading journal

Open a book to see its details and story. The Yours pages hold your private rating and notes. You can organize your reading, choose a cover, archive a book, and restore archived books from your shelf.

Choose a library

In Settings, choose your library and, where available, your home branch. Location permission is optional and helps sort nearby libraries. Library features vary: some catalogs provide availability and account activity, others provide availability only, and some listed libraries are not supported yet. The app shows that distinction when you choose a library.

Connect your library card

At libraries that support account sync, you can optionally enter your card number and PIN in Settings to see checkouts, holds, due dates, and pickup information. You do not need a myBookshelf account to scan books or use your personal shelf.

If a connection stops working, check that your card and PIN work on the library’s official website, then use Reconnect in the app. A library can change its catalog or access rules. Borrowing, renewals, and hold actions may open the library’s website.

Reminders and availability

Allow notifications to receive local due-date and pickup reminders. If you previously declined, you can enable notifications for the app in iPhone Settings. Refresh your library activity when you need the latest status. Background updates depend on the time iOS allows; reminders and cached availability are a convenience, and your library’s official account remains the source for borrowing deadlines.

Privacy and moving devices

Your native bookshelf, notes, and library connection are stored on your device. The app does not provide account-based synchronization between phones. Read the privacy policy for details about credentials, optional permissions, operating-system backups, and deletion.

Erase or disconnect

Signing out disconnects your selected library. Settings → Erase everything clears the app’s local bookshelf, ratings, notes, library credentials, and reminders. This cannot be undone. Archive a book instead if you want to restore it later.
