Help with your reading shelf

For phones and computers. Your reading shelf is at mybookshelf.cloud.

Find a book

Type a title, author or ISBN in Search for a book at the top of your reading shelf. On a phone you can also tap the barcode button in the search bar and point the camera at the barcode on the back of the book. Open a book and choose Add to my reading shelf to keep it.

Your reading shelf also suggests books, marked Suggested. Open one to see it, and add it if you like it.

Camera not opening?

Allow camera access for mybookshelf.cloud in your browser’s site settings, then try again. Barcode reading happens on your phone; camera images are not sent anywhere. You can always type the ISBN instead.

Choose your library

On a phone, your library is shown above your books, with Change beside it. On a computer, it is at the top of the column on the left, with Change library. Choose it, then type a city, town or library name, or choose Libraries near me. Your location is used once to sort the list and is not stored.

If your library is not in the list, choose My library isn’t listed and tell us its name and city. Nothing is sent until you press Send request.

What the answers mean

Under each book on your reading shelf, and on the book’s page, MyBookshelf says in words what your library’s catalog told us:

  • In your library: the catalog lists this book. Open the book and use See this book in the library’s catalog to check copies and borrow it through the library.
  • Not in your library: we looked in the catalog and did not find this book. The library may still have another edition; Search the library’s catalog yourself opens its website.
  • Couldn’t check right now: the catalog did not answer in time. This does not mean the book is missing. Try again later, or open the catalog.
  • Checking your library…: we are asking the catalog now. A slow catalog can take a little while, and you can leave the page meanwhile.

The answers are for the library you chose. Choose another library and the books are checked again.

Settings

On a phone, the settings gear at the top right of your reading shelf opens Settings: your library, your account, the reading light, and help, privacy and about. On a computer, the same things are in the column on the left: your library, your account, Settings and Help.

Your account (optional)

You do not need an account. Without one, your reading shelf stays in this browser, and clearing the browser’s site data removes it. A free account keeps the same reading shelf, library and the books you viewed on your phone and your computer.

To create one, open Settings, choose Create account and enter your email and a password. We email you a link: open it and you are signed in.

To log in, choose Log in, enter your email and choose Email me a log-in link. Open the link on the same device and you are logged in, with no password needed. You can also log in with your password; if you forgot it, choose Forgot your password?.

When you sign in, the books already on your reading shelf in this browser join your account, so nothing is lost. If this browser and your account have different libraries, your account’s library is kept.

In Your account you can change your email or password, Log out on this device (your reading shelf stays safe in your account), or delete your account. Deleting it removes your email, password, reading shelf, library and the books you viewed, and clears what MyBookshelf keeps in this browser.

Report a problem

Choose Report a problem in Settings (on a computer, under Help) and tell us what went wrong. The screen you were on, your library and the book go with your message. Your name and email are optional, for a reply, and we delete them after 90 days.

Need a hand? Email privacy@mybookshelf.cloud with your phone or computer, your browser and what happened. Please don’t send passwords, log-in links, or library card numbers and PINs.

The earlier MyBookshelf iPhone and Android apps are archived. What they did with your information is still described in the privacy policy.
