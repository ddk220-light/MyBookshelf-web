Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Your native app data stays with you. Your shelf, notes, ratings, and library activity are stored on your device. The native apps use no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. You do not create an account with myBookshelf.

This policy covers the myBookshelf native apps for iPhone and Android (also known as MyBookshelf). Website privacy is described below. The apps are developed by Deepak Ravikumar.

Website privacy

Website section updated: November 1, 2026.

The website lets you find books, save a bookshelf, and check library catalogs without signing in. It has no sign-in, library-card fields, or access to your native app’s shelf, notes, ratings, checkouts, or holds.

Your library selection, recently viewed books, saved web shelf (including free books and papers), and appearance and companion preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage. Search terms are not saved there. Web lookups send search terms, library selections, and book or paper identifiers to the website’s server so it can obtain public catalog information. Cover images load from approved public providers.

Free-book searches use imported public metadata. Paper searches are sent through the server to selected research indexes, such as Europe PMC, DOAJ, arXiv or OpenAlex. Temporary shared search and record caches reduce repeated requests and enforce source limits; they do not contain your saved shelf. Reading links open the original host, whose privacy policy applies there.

Barcode scanning happens on your device. Camera images are not uploaded.

If you choose Use my location, the website sends your coordinates to its server to find nearby libraries. They are kept only in the open tab’s memory, not in saved browser preferences or application telemetry. City and postcode searches are sent through our server to Photon (Komoot), which uses OpenStreetMap data. Opening the map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap; that service receives your IP address, the website origin, and the map areas you view. You can use library-name and area search without granting location access or opening the map.

The hosting service processes ordinary network and operational logs, which may include an IP address and request details. The website does not run advertising or analytics scripts. You can clear your saved shelf in Settings, or remove all stored preferences with your browser’s site-data controls.

Books and personal library activity

Books you save, your reading status, private ratings, notes, and archived books are stored in the app’s local database. If you connect a supported library account, its checkouts, holds, due dates, and pickup status are retrieved directly from the library or its catalog provider and cached on your device. The developer does not receive this information.

Library card and PIN

Library sign-in is optional. A card number and PIN you enter are sent over HTTPS directly to your chosen library’s catalog service to authenticate and retrieve your library activity. They are never sent to a myBookshelf server.

On iPhone, credentials are stored in the device Keychain with device-only protection and are not synchronized through iCloud Keychain. On Android, credentials are encrypted using an Android Keystore-protected key. The app uses the library session only to provide the features you request.

Book lookups and external services

To find a book’s cover, description, publication details, or available reading preview, the app sends an ISBN or your title/author search to Google Books and Open Library. Open Library cover images may be delivered by the Internet Archive. To check borrowing availability, it sends book identifiers or search terms to your selected catalog service, which may use BiblioCommons, Calil, or Aspen Discovery.

Cover images load from the book and catalog providers. These services receive the network requests, including an IP address, and handle them under their own privacy policies. The app does not attach your library card, PIN, private notes, or private ratings to book-metadata requests.

Links to Goodreads, book previews, and library websites open the relevant website. Interactions there are governed by that website’s policies. myBookshelf is an independent app and is not operated by those services or by your library.

Camera

The camera is used to recognize book barcodes while you use the Scan screen. Barcode recognition happens on your device. Camera images are not uploaded or saved by the app. You can search by title, author, or ISBN without granting camera access.

Location and maps

If you choose to find libraries near you, the app requests location permission and a location fix. Your coordinate is used on the device to sort the bundled library directory. The app does not send that coordinate to the developer or library catalogs. You can choose a library by name without granting location access.

Maps use Apple Maps on iPhone and the device’s map service on Android. Map-service requests are handled by the relevant provider under its privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders using due dates and pickup information available on your device. It does not register you with a developer-operated push-notification server. Library refreshes depend on connectivity and the background time the operating system allows.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Local data remains until you change or erase it. Archiving a book hides it from active shelves and lets you restore it later. Signing out removes the selected library’s stored credentials and its session.

To erase the native app’s saved books, notes, ratings, library connections, and reminders, use Settings → Erase everything. Use this before uninstalling if you want to ensure that Keychain credentials are removed too; Keychain items can remain after an iPhone app is uninstalled.

The app has no account-based cloud synchronization. Your operating system’s device backup may include local app data under your Apple or Google backup settings. Device-only iPhone Keychain credentials cannot be restored onto another device.

Tracking and children

The native apps contain no advertising or analytics SDKs and do not track you across apps or websites. The developer does not sell your data. The app is not directed specifically to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
