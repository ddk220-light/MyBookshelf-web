Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short. You never need an account. Without one, your reading shelf stays in your browser. Apart from the random number and approximate place that Google Analytics keeps, we keep no IP addresses, device identifiers or location in our records. We never link any log to an account, show no advertising and never sell information.

MyBookshelf is run by Deepak Ravikumar, India. This page covers the MyBookshelf website and web app at mybookshelf.cloud, then the archived MyBookshelf apps for iPhone and Android.

Website privacy

Your account (optional)

If you create an account, we keep your email address, a scrambled form of your password (never the password itself), your reading shelf, your chosen library and your Viewed before list (the newest 50 books you opened while signed in). You can log in with a link we email you instead of your password: the link works once, only for 15 minutes. We use your email address for log-in links, to confirm your address, and for security and account messages, never for marketing.

Information stored in your browser

Without an account, your reading shelf, Viewed before list, chosen library and reading light stay in your own browser. When you sign in, the books on your reading shelf in that browser are added to your account, and the browser remembers which ones it has added. If the browser and your account have different libraries, your account’s library is kept. Signed-in cookies keep you logged in and hold no reading data.

Library checks, searches and usage

To show whether your library has your books and to suggest books, the app sends us your chosen library and the identifiers of some of your books; we use them for that answer only. When we check a library, we keep its answer (found or not found) for that library and book for six months; a failed check is never stored as an answer.

When you search, we record the words you searched for, the library involved, what happened, how many results came back and how long it took, in an anonymous log kept for 12 months and then reduced to totals. We keep no account ID, IP address or device identifier with it, and search words are never put in web addresses. None of this is linked to an account or a person.

Google Analytics, through the Google tag, counts visits and a few actions, such as searching, choosing a library, adding a book and checking a library. Google receives the addresses and titles of the pages you open (a book’s page included), how you arrived, the links you follow to other sites, the type of device and browser, your approximate location, the library you chose or checked, and whether a search or check found anything; never your search words, your account or your precise location. It sets cookies that hold a random number for your browser and last up to two years after your visit. The counts are kept up to 14 months, then only totals. You can block or delete the cookies. To stop the counting altogether, block the script or use Google’s opt-out add-on (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout); MyBookshelf works fully without it.

Library requests and problem reports

If you ask us to add a library or report a problem, we keep what you wrote and, for a report, the screen, library and book shown. You may add your name and email so that we can reply; both are optional, and we delete them after 90 days.

Location, camera and abuse protection

“Libraries near me” sends your location to our server once, to sort the list; we do not store it. Towns and cities you type are looked up through our server with Photon (Komoot), using OpenStreetMap data. The barcode scanner works on your device; camera images are not sent to us or saved. To limit misuse, we keep a scrambled code made from your IP address for up to two days.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the website and server; Neon holds our databases; Resend sends account email; Open Library, the Internet Archive and Google Books provide book details and covers; library catalogs (or their providers, such as BiblioCommons or Calil) answer whether they have a book; Google Analytics counts visits; Google Search Console shows how people find MyBookshelf in Google Search. These services receive normal network information, such as an IP address, and may keep short-term logs under their own policies.

Your choices

Change your email or password, log out, or delete your account in Settings, then Account. Deleting your account removes your email, password, reading shelf, chosen library and Viewed before list, and clears what MyBookshelf keeps in that browser, except Google Analytics cookies; live data is removed within 24 hours and backups expire within 30 days. You can clear browser data through your browser’s site data settings. To ask for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, write to privacy@mybookshelf.cloud. You must be 18 or older, or have a parent’s or guardian’s permission, to use MyBookshelf.

This part covers the archived MyBookshelf apps for iPhone and Android.

Books and personal library activity

Books you save, your reading status, private ratings, notes, and archived books are stored in the app’s local database. If you connect a supported library account, its checkouts, holds, due dates, and pickup status are retrieved directly from the library or its catalog provider and cached on your device. The developer does not receive this information.

Library card and PIN

Library sign-in is optional. A card number and PIN you enter are sent over HTTPS directly to your chosen library’s catalog service to authenticate and retrieve your library activity. They are never sent to a MyBookshelf server.

On iPhone, credentials are stored in the device Keychain with device-only protection and are not synchronized through iCloud Keychain. On Android, credentials are encrypted using an Android Keystore-protected key. The app uses the library session only to provide the features you request.

Book lookups and external services

To find a book’s cover, description, publication details, or available reading preview, the app sends an ISBN or your title/author search to Google Books and Open Library. Open Library cover images may be delivered by the Internet Archive. To check borrowing availability, it sends book identifiers or search terms to your selected catalog service, which may use BiblioCommons, Calil, or Aspen Discovery.

Cover images load from the book and catalog providers. These services receive the network requests, including an IP address, and handle them under their own privacy policies. The app does not attach your library card, PIN, private notes, or private ratings to book-metadata requests.

Links to Goodreads, book previews, and library websites open the relevant website. Interactions there are governed by that website’s policies. MyBookshelf is an independent app and is not operated by those services or by your library.

Camera

The camera is used to recognize book barcodes while you use the Scan screen. Barcode recognition happens on your device. Camera images are not uploaded or saved by the app. You can search by title, author, or ISBN without granting camera access.

Location and maps

If you choose to find libraries near you, the app requests location permission and a location fix. Your coordinate is used on the device to sort the bundled library directory. The app does not send that coordinate to the developer or library catalogs. You can choose a library by name without granting location access.

Maps use Apple Maps on iPhone and the device’s map service on Android. Map-service requests are handled by the relevant provider under its privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders using due dates and pickup information available on your device. It does not register you with a developer-operated push-notification server. Library refreshes depend on connectivity and the background time the operating system allows.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Local data remains until you change or erase it. Archiving a book hides it from active shelves and lets you restore it later. Signing out removes the selected library’s stored credentials and its session.

To erase the native app’s saved books, notes, ratings, library connections, and reminders, use Settings → Erase everything. Use this before uninstalling if you want to ensure that Keychain credentials are removed too; Keychain items can remain after an iPhone app is uninstalled.

The app has no account-based cloud synchronization. Your operating system’s device backup may include local app data under your Apple or Google backup settings. Device-only iPhone Keychain credentials cannot be restored onto another device.

Tracking and children

The native apps contain no advertising or analytics SDKs and do not track you across apps or websites. The developer does not sell your data. The app is not directed specifically to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date. For privacy questions or to report a concern, contact privacy@mybookshelf.cloud.
